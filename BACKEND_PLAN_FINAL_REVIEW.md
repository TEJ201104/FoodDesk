     - **Single-Stall Business Model:** Designed for individual kiosk, food truck, or independent quick-service operators who run exactly one stall under their account.      - **Multi-Tenant Cloud SaaS (Default Mode):** Centralized cloud infrastructure hosting multiple independent businesses and stalls, with strict tenant isolation enforced at the database and application levels.

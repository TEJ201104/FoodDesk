1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Login and Authentication Gate is the primary security entrance to the FoodDesk platform. It provides a secure, role-segmented interface where users verify their identity before accessing stall operations or platform governance.

FoodDesk features a unique Dual-Mode Verification Gate:
- Stall Owner Mode: A specialized 3-credential verification gate (Email Address, Password, and authorized Stall ID) designed to ensure staff explicitly select and lock into their intended physical stall counter upon opening the POS.
- Super Admin Portal: A platform governance gate requiring 2 credentials (Email Address and Password) that grants access to platform-wide stall approvals, owner directories, and support desk tickets.

In addition to standard credential authentication, the screen provides 1-click demonstration autofill shortcuts, immediate inline validation, a visual background ambiance with ambient glowing highlights, and direct navigation links to public customer portals.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Entry URL: http://localhost:5173/login
- Default System Entry Point: Accessing the root URL http://localhost:5173/ while unauthenticated automatically redirects to /login via the top-level route redirect in AppRoutes.jsx.
- Guard Redirects: Any attempt by an unauthenticated user to access protected routes (such as /dashboard, /pos, /orders, /kds, or /admin/dashboard) is intercepted by AuthGuard and redirected to /login with state.from preserved.

6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: LOGGING IN AS A STALL OWNER (MANUAL)
1. Navigate to http://localhost:5173/login.
2. Confirm that the "Stall Owner Login" tab is highlighted (active by default).
3. In the "Registered Email Address" field, enter your authorized email (e.g., owner1@gmail.com).
4. In the "Password" field, enter your account password (e.g., owner1).
5. In the "Unique Stall ID" field, enter the stall identifier you wish to manage (e.g., stall-1).
6. Click "Verify Credentials & Open POS" (or press Enter).
7. System displays a green success toast: "Welcome, Owner 1! Accessing Fast Food Stall." and redirects to /dashboard.

SCENARIO B: LOGGING IN USING 1-CLICK DEMO BUTTONS
1. Scroll down to the "1-Click Demonstration Credentials" section.
2. Click "Owner 1: Fast Food".
3. Notice that the email, password, and stall ID fields are immediately filled with valid data.
4. Click "Verify Credentials & Open POS".
5. You are instantly authenticated into the Fast Food Stall workspace.

SCENARIO C: LOGGING IN AS SUPER ADMIN
1. Click the "Super Admin Portal" tab button at the top of the card.
2. Notice that the "Unique Stall ID" field disappears, the badge updates to "Admin", and the button changes to "Sign In as Super Admin".

7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Client-Side Credential Verification:
  The form delegates authentication to mockAuthService.loginStallOwner() or mockAuthService.loginSuperAdmin().
- Stall Authorization Check:
  In Stall Owner mode, mockAuthService verifies that:
  1. The user exists in the initialUsers directory.
  2. The password matches the user's registered password.
  3. The requested stallId is included in the user's authorizedStalls array.
  4. The requested stall exists and has an 'active' status in the stalls registry.
- Session Storage Mutation:
  Upon successful authentication, mockAuthService constructs a session object containing user details, the active stall profile, and a mock JWT token (mock-jwt-token-stall_owner-...). This object is written to localStorage under the key 'fooddesk_session'.
- React Context Update:
  AuthContext updates its in-memory session state, triggering re-renders in all subscribed layout components (Navbar, Sidebar).
- Navigation Redirection:
  The router transitions the browser to location.state.from.pathname (if the user was intercepted) or defaults to /dashboard for stall owners and /admin/dashboard for super admins.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Exact Stall ID Matching:
  Stall IDs must match the canonical registered IDs (stall-1, stall-2, stall-3). The input field automatically normalizes entered characters to lowercase to prevent accidental casing mismatches.
- Single-Stall vs. Multi-Stall Authorization:
  Owner 1 is authorized for both stall-1 and stall-2. Owner 2 is authorized exclusively for stall-3. Owner 2 cannot log in to stall-1 or stall-2 under any circumstances.
- Unauthenticated Public Safety:
  Public menu (/menu/:stallSlug) and tracking (/track/:stallSlug) pages do not require authentication and can be accessed without creating an active session.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why does a stall owner need to provide three credentials instead of just an email and password?
A1: FoodDesk supports multi-stall ownership. An entrepreneur may operate several food counters (e.g., Fast Food and Beverages). Requiring the Stall ID at login ensures the counter terminal starts immediately in the correct physical workspace without ambiguity.

Q2: Can I switch stalls after logging in without logging out?
A2: Yes, if your account is authorized for multiple stalls (like Owner 1). Once logged in, use the dynamic "Switch Active Stall" dropdown in the top navigation bar to switch between authorized counters with 1 click. Single-stall owners (like Owner 2) are locked to their single assigned stall.

Q3: What happens if I type an incorrect password multiple times?
A3: FoodDesk displays an immediate error notification for each failed attempt. In the demonstration build, there is no account lockout timer, allowing rapid testing and demonstration.

Q4: Does logging in on one tab log me in across other open browser tabs?
A4: Yes. FoodDesk uses persistent browser localStorage. Any other open tabs will detect the updated session upon user interaction or reload.

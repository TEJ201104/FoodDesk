1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Role Access and Navigation is the structural backbone of FoodDesk, controlling how authenticated users explore the application, switch between operational modules, and transition between multi-stall workspaces.

The platform provides a strictly segregated navigation shell tailored dynamically to the authenticated identity:
- For Stall Owners: A comprehensive 10-item operational sidebar (Dashboard, POS, Orders, KDS, Menu, Expenses, Analytics, Inventory, Support, Settings), an active stall indicator with dynamic 1-click workspace switching (for multi-stall owners), and quick links to public customer menus.
- For Super Admins: A focused 5-item governance sidebar (Dashboard, Stall Approvals, Owner Management, Issue Management, Platform Settings) with elevated platform status badges.

The navigation system is protected by three interlocking route guards (AuthGuard, RoleGuard, and StallGuard) that intercept unauthorized transitions, prevent role crossover, and self-heal client-side storage discrepancies.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Entry Requirement: An active authenticated session (Stall Owner or Super Admin).
- Component Mounting: Automatically wraps all internal application routes via AppLayout.jsx, which mounts the persistent top Navbar and collapsible Sidebar around the routed page view.


3. SCREEN LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application layout uses a modern, responsive, two-part persistent shell:

1. Top Navigation Bar (Navbar.jsx):
   A fixed-height (h-16, 64px) sticky header spanning the entire width of the viewport. Positioned at z-index 30, it features brand identity, live Asia/Kolkata clock, active stall preview or switcher, audio chime diagnostic button, user profile avatar pill, and the safe logout button.

2. Side Navigation Drawer (Sidebar.jsx):
   A fixed-width (w-64, 256px) panel positioned below the Navbar along the left edge. On desktop screens (>= 1024px), it is permanently pinned. On mobile and tablet screens (< 1024px), it operates as a slide-out drawer with a darkened backdrop, toggled via the hamburger icon in the Navbar.

3. Main Scrollable Content Area:
   The central canvas occupying the remaining viewport space (lg:pl-64). Protected by RootErrorBoundary, this area dynamically renders the active page component.

  - For Super Admin:

  - Location: Center-right in the Navbar.

5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigate to Module:
   Click any sidebar link to transition to that operational screen. The active route is highlighted in solid brand orange with an elevated drop shadow.
2. Toggle Mobile Drawer:
   On mobile/tablet screens, tap the hamburger icon in the Navbar to open the menu. Tap any link or click the darkened backdrop to close the drawer.
3. Switch Active Stall (Multi-Stall Owners):
   Click the stall switcher in the Navbar or the quick-switch buttons in the Sidebar banner to instantly change workspaces.
4. Test Sound Feedback:
   Click the speaker icon in the Navbar to test counter audio responsiveness.
5. Launch Customer Portals:
   Click "Customer Menu" in the Navbar or either link under "Customer Portals" in the Sidebar to preview walk-in guest screens.
6. Terminate Session:
   Click the "Logout" button in the top right to safely exit the application.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: SWITCHING BETWEEN WORKSPACES (MULTI-STALL OWNER)
1. Log in as Owner 1 with active stall stall-1 (Fast Food).
2. Look at the top Navbar: notice the pill displaying "Fast Food Stall (stall-1)".
3. Click the stall pill button. A dropdown menu opens showing:
   - "Fast Food Stall (stall-1 • Fast Food & Snacks)" with an active checkmark.
   - "Beverages Stall (stall-2 • Tea, Coffee & Cold Beverages)".
4. Click "Beverages Stall".
5. The dropdown closes, a green toast appears: "Switched active workspace to Beverages Stall", and the active stall badge updates to Beverages Stall (stall-2).
6. Notice that the entire screen immediately updates: the POS menu catalog, dashboard metrics, orders table, and expenses now display Beverages Stall data.

SCENARIO B: ATTEMPTING UNAUTHORIZED ROLE CROSSOVER (STALL OWNER TO ADMIN)
1. Log in as a Stall Owner.
2. In your browser's address bar, manually type: http://localhost:5173/admin/dashboard.
3. Press Enter.
4. Notice that RoleGuard intercepts the navigation. You are NOT allowed into the admin area; instead, the browser automatically bounces you back to http://localhost:5173/dashboard.

SCENARIO C: MOBILE NAVIGATION DRAWER
1. Resize your browser window to a mobile width (< 1024px).
2. Notice that the sidebar disappears and a hamburger icon appears in the top-left Navbar.
3. Tap the hamburger icon. The sidebar slides smoothly into view from the left with a backdrop.
4. Tap "Kitchen Display (KDS)".
5. The drawer automatically closes and the browser navigates to the KDS page.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Active Route Highlighting:
  Sidebar uses NavLink with an isActive callback. When active, className applies bg-brand-500 text-white font-semibold shadow-sm. Inactive items use text-slate-300 hover:text-white hover:bg-navy-900.
- Reactive Stall Switching:
  When switchStall(targetStallId) is called:
  1. StallContext verifies that targetStallId is authorized for the current user.
  2. mockAuthService updates the activeStall in the stored session object.
  3. StallContext increments stallChangeKey by 1.
  4. Every component subscribed to stallChangeKey (Dashboard, POS, Orders, Analytics, etc.) recomputes its data queries immediately.
  5. The POS cart is cleared to prevent cross-stall food item contamination.
- StallGuard Runtime Inspection:
  On every route transition, StallGuard verifies that currentStall.id belongs to authorizedStalls. If a discrepancy exists, it automatically executes recoverAuthorizedStall().

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Locked Single-Stall Accounts:
  Owner 2 is authorized exclusively for stall-3. Owner 2 will never see a dropdown arrow or quick-switch buttons; their workspace is strictly locked to Desserts & Sweets.
- Super Admin Restrictions:
  Super Admins do not operate individual stalls. If a Super Admin navigates to /orders or /kds, an explicit governance notice is displayed.
- Zero Cart Contamination:
  When switching stalls, any items currently in the POS cart are automatically discarded. An operator cannot add a Burger from stall-1 and checkout with a Tea from stall-2 in the same order ticket.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why are there 10 items in the stall sidebar and only 5 in the admin sidebar?
A1: Stall owners manage the complete operational lifecycle of a business (taking orders, cooking in the kitchen, tracking inventory, paying expenses, analyzing profits, and customer service). Super Admins have a governance role (approving stalls, managing owners, resolving platform tickets, and platform settings).

Q2: What is the "HOT" badge on the POS navigation item?
A2: The "HOT" badge indicates that POS / New Order is the primary, highest-frequency workstation used continuously throughout the business day.

Q3: Does switching stalls log me out?
A3: No. Switching stalls updates your active workspace in memory and storage seamlessly in less than 5 milliseconds without requiring re-authentication.

Q4: What timezone does the top clock display?
A4: It displays Asia/Kolkata (Indian Standard Time, IST) regardless of the local computer's system clock, ensuring consistent timestamping across food court branches.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Stall Dashboard (/dashboard) is the executive operations cockpit and default landing page for authenticated Stall Owners.

Upon opening the workstation, the dashboard provides a consolidated, high-level executive summary of the stall's performance for the current business day. It eliminates traditional end-of-day cash reconciliation headaches by aggregating sales, order queue statuses, verified payment collections, active inventory alerts, and estimated net profit in real time.

Key capabilities include:
- A prominent Hero Welcome Banner identifying the active stall name, ID, and category with quick one-click workstation launch buttons.
- Real-time Asia/Kolkata scoped business metrics computed dynamically for the active stall workspace.
- Four core operational and financial KPI cards: Gross Sales (Today), Total Orders (All-Time), Verified Payments Rate, and Estimated Net Profit (Today).
- An architectural compliance verification card confirming that Multi-Stall Data Isolation and StallGuard runtime protection are active.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct URL: http://localhost:5173/dashboard
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "Dashboard" (the first item with the LayoutDashboard icon) in the stall navigation sidebar.
- Brand Emblem: Clicking the "FoodDesk" logo in the top Navbar while logged in as a Stall Owner navigates directly to /dashboard.
- Post-Login Redirect: Stall Owners are automatically forwarded to this screen immediately after successful authentication at the Login Gate.

7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dynamic Calculation Hook:
  StallDashboardPage uses a React useMemo hook that executes whenever currentStall.id or stallChangeKey changes:
  1. Calls stallDataService.getStallSummary(currentStall.id) to retrieve queue counts, menu item count, and payment method distribution.
  2. Calls analyticsService.getStallFinancialAnalytics(currentStall.id, 'today') to retrieve authoritative PRD Section 11 financial metrics strictly bounded by Asia/Kolkata today's date string.
  3. Combines operational and financial objects into a single summary state.
- Zero Server Latency:
  Because calculations are executed in-memory against local storage data, the entire dashboard recomputes in under 3 milliseconds.
- Error Handling:
  If a stall ID is uninitialized or invalid, a warning alert box is rendered: "Unable to calculate metrics for this stall. Please ensure your stall session is active and refresh."

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Scope Boundary:
  The "Gross Sales" and "Est. Net Profit" cards on this dashboard are strictly scoped to TODAY in the Asia/Kolkata timezone. Historical weekly or monthly aggregates must be viewed on the Financial Analytics page (/analytics).
- Realized Revenue Only:
  Unpaid orders, pending orders, and cancelled orders with no payment are strictly excluded from Gross Sales and Net Profit.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why does the Dashboard say "Est. Net Profit" instead of just "Net Profit"?
A1: Because it calculates real-time profit for TODAY based on sales, frozen item cost prices (COGS), and expenses recorded so far today. Final daily profit may adjust if evening expenses (such as daily labor or closing waste) are logged before shutdown.

Q2: How often do the metrics refresh?
A2: Instantly. Whenever an order is created at the POS, payment is recorded, or an expense is added, custom events notify the application and update the dashboard automatically.

Q3: Can a Super Admin view this dashboard?
A3: No. The Stall Dashboard is reserved for authorized stall staff. If a Super Admin attempts to access /dashboard, RoleGuard automatically redirects them to /admin/dashboard.

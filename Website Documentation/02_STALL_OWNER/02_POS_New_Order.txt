1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Point of Sale (POS) / New Order Workstation (/pos) is the primary, high-velocity counter interface of FoodDesk. It is engineered specifically for fast, walk-in counter ordering environments where cashiers must take orders, apply discounts, collect payments, and dispatch kitchen tickets in under 15 seconds.

Key capabilities include:
- An ultra-fast split-screen layout: a responsive Food Catalog Grid on the left (8 columns on desktop) paired with a sticky Interactive Cart & Bill Summary on the right (4 columns on desktop).
- Live real-time search across item names, categories, and ingredients.
- Dynamic category filter pills with real-time item count indicators.
- High-visibility product cards with dietary veg/non-veg indicators, selling prices, and active sold-out protection (disables ordering of out-of-stock items).
- Real-time cart calculations supporting quantity increment/decrement, percentage discounts, flat rupee discounts, customer mobile capture, and kitchen special preparation notes.
- Atomic order number generation (e.g., ORD-20261116-0001) and prominent 3-digit pickup tokens (e.g., Token #101).
- Integrated Order Review Modal, Success Modal, Payment Collection Modal, and Digital Thermal Receipt Modal.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct URL: http://localhost:5173/pos
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "POS / New Order" (the second item with the ShoppingBag icon and orange "HOT" badge).
- Dashboard Shortcut: Click the primary orange "Open POS Workstation" button on the Stall Dashboard (/dashboard).


3. SCREEN LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The POS screen is organized into three major functional areas:

1. Top Workstation Bar:
   A clean navy header containing active stall identification, workspace breadcrumbs ("Walk-in POS Workstation"), headline ("Counter Point of Sale"), and an instant search input with a clear button.

2. Left Section — Category Navigation & Food Menu Grid (8 cols on lg):
   - Category Filter Pills Bar: A horizontally scrollable row of rounded category filter buttons showing item counts.
   - Food Menu Grid: A responsive multi-column grid of food cards displaying image thumbnails, dietary veg/non-veg pills, item names, prices, and "Add to Cart" or quantity counter controls.

3. Right Section — Sticky Cart Sidebar & Billing Ledger (4 cols on lg):
   A permanently visible, sticky billing column that follows the cashier as they scroll. It contains the cart header with item count and "Clear Cart" button, scrollable order line-items, customer mobile input, special instructions textarea, discount selector (None, Flat, Percentage), financial bill breakdown (Subtotal, Discount, Grand Total), and the primary "Review & Place Order" button.


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every single interactive element on the POS page is cataloged below:

A. WORKSTATION TOP BAR:
- Stall Context Badge:
  - Text: Store icon + Active stall name (e.g., "Fast Food Stall") + Stall ID font-mono in parentheses "(stall-1)".
- Subtitle: "Walk-in POS Workstation".
- Workstation Title: "Counter Point of Sale" (text-2xl font-black).
- Live Search Input:
  - Location: Right side of top bar.
  - Placeholder: "Search items or ingredients...".
  - Behavior: Filters food cards in real time as the cashier types.
  - Clear Button: 'X' icon appears when text is entered; clicking it instantly clears the search query.

B. CATEGORY FILTER PILLS (POSCategoryFilter.jsx):
- "All Items" Pill:
  - Displays total number of active menu items for the stall (e.g., "All (10)").
- Category Pills (e.g., "Burgers", "Rolls", "Combos", "Beverages"):
  - Dynamically generated from the stall's menu catalog.
  - Each pill displays the category title and an item count badge.
  - Active state: Solid brand orange background with white text and drop shadow.

C. FOOD ITEM CARDS (POSMenuGrid.jsx):
- Item Thumbnail Image:
  - Displays food photograph with fallback image placeholder on image load failure.
- Dietary Pill (Top Left):
  - Green dot with "VEG" or red dot with "NON-VEG".
- Category Badge (Top Right):
  - Semi-transparent dark pill showing the item category.
- Item Name & Description:
  - High-contrast, bold title (line-clamp-1) and short description.
- Selling Price:
  - Formatted in Indian Rupees (e.g., "₹120.00") in bold orange.
- Sold-Out Overlay & State:
  - If item.isAvailable === false, the card is visually dimmed (opacity-50), a prominent "Sold Out" watermark covers the thumbnail, and the action button is replaced with a disabled "Sold Out" pill. Attempting to add it triggers a warning toast.
- Add to Cart Button:
  - When quantity is 0: Full-width button with Plus icon reading "Add".
  - When quantity > 0: Transforms into an active counter showing minus (-) button, bold quantity number, and plus (+) button.

D. CART SIDEBAR (POSCartSidebar.jsx):
- Cart Header:
  - Title: "Current Order Ticket" with cart item counter badge.
  - "Clear Cart" Button: Trash2 icon button. Discards all cart items and resets discounts/inputs.
- Cart Line-Items List:
  - Scrollable container displaying each added item:
    - Item Name & Category.
    - Unit Price (e.g., "@ ₹120 each").
    - Quantity Stepper: [-] decrement button, quantity text, [+] increment button. Decrementing below 1 removes the item.
    - Line Total: Bold rupee amount (sellingPrice * quantity).
    - Remove Button: Red trash icon to remove the item immediately.
- Customer Mobile Number Input:
  - Field: <input type="tel"> with Phone icon.
  - Placeholder: "Customer Mobile (Optional)".
  - Format: Allows 10-digit Indian mobile numbers. Used for receipt SMS/WhatsApp and customer lookup.
- Customer Preparation Notes:
  - Field: <textarea rows="2"> with FileText icon.
  - Placeholder: "Special instructions (e.g. extra spicy, no onion)...".
  - Destination: Forwarded directly to the Kitchen Display System (KDS) card and printed on thermal receipts.
- Discount Controls:
  - Selector: Three segmented buttons: "None", "₹ Flat", "% Percent".
  - Value Input: Appears when Flat or Percentage is selected.
    - For Percentage: Enforces a cap of 100%.
    - For Flat: Enforces a cap equal to the cart subtotal (cannot produce a negative total).
- Financial Summary Breakdown:
  - Subtotal: Sum of item prices * quantities.
  - Discount: Green text displaying "-₹XX.00" if active.
  - Grand Total: Extra-large, high-contrast bold amount (e.g., "₹340.00").
- Primary Action Button:
  - Label: "Review & Place Order" (or "Cart is Empty" if no items).
  - Disabled State: Disabled when cart has 0 items.
  - Action: Opens POSOrderReviewModal.

E. ORDER REVIEW MODAL (POSOrderReviewModal.jsx):
- Pre-submission confirmation dialogue verifying all order details before creating the record:
  - Shows preview of upcoming Order Number and Pickup Token (e.g., "Next Order: ORD-20261116-0005 • Token #105").
  - Table of all items, quantities, and line totals.
  - Customer mobile and special notes.
  - Payment method selector: "Cash" or "UPI / Online".
  - Financial summary: Subtotal, Discount, Grand Total.
  - "Back to Edit" Button: Closes modal and returns to cart.
  - "Confirm & Dispatch Order" Button: Creates order in storage, plays success chime, and opens Success Modal.

F. ORDER SUCCESS MODAL (POSOrderSuccessModal.jsx):
- Full-screen success celebration modal:
  - Emerald check animation with headline "Order Created Successfully!".
  - Giant Pickup Token Box: Displays "TOKEN" and the 3-digit token (e.g., "#105") in 48px bold font for calling out to the customer.
  - Order Number & Stall info: "ORD-20261116-0005 • Fast Food Stall".
  - Action Buttons:
    1. "Collect Payment Now" (Emerald Button): Opens POSPaymentModal to record cash or UPI transfer.
    2. "View / Print Receipt" (Navy Button): Opens DigitalThermalReceiptModal.
    3. "Start Next Order" (Primary Button): Clears cart and resets workstation for the next customer in line.

G. PAYMENT MODAL (POSPaymentModal.jsx):
- Dedicated tender collection dialogue (covered in detail in 05_END_TO_END_WORKFLOWS/03_Payment_and_Receipt_Workflow.txt).
  - Cash Mode: Amount received input + live Change Due calculator.
  - UPI Mode: Dynamic UPI QR code preview with stall UPI VPA + Transaction ID input.
  - "Mark as Paid" Button: Dispatches payment-completed event.

H. DIGITAL THERMAL RECEIPT MODAL (DigitalThermalReceiptModal.jsx):
- Authentic 58mm / 80mm thermal receipt preview:
  - Stall header, address, contact, tax invoice header.
  - Pickup Token # and Order Number.
  - Itemized table with quantities, prices, subtotal, discount, grand total.
  - Payment status and tender type.
  - "Print Receipt" Button: Triggers native browser print dialog (window.print()).


5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Search Catalog: Type in the search box to find items by name or ingredient.
2. Filter by Category: Click any category pill to isolate specific menu sections.
3. Add Item to Cart: Click "Add" on any available food card.
4. Modify Quantities: Use [+] and [-] buttons on the card or in the cart sidebar.
5. Remove Item: Click the trash icon next to any cart line item.
6. Clear Entire Cart: Click the top trash icon in the cart header.
7. Apply Discount: Select Flat (₹) or Percentage (%) and enter discount value.
8. Enter Customer Details: Add customer mobile number and kitchen instructions.
9. Review Order: Click "Review & Place Order" to open the verification modal.
10. Confirm Order: Click "Confirm & Dispatch Order" to assign token and dispatch to KDS.
11. Collect Payment: Process cash change or scan UPI QR.
12. Print Thermal Receipt: Print physical receipt on counter thermal printer.
13. Start Next Order: Reset workstation immediately for the next customer.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO: RECORDING A STANDARD WALK-IN COUNTER ORDER
1. A customer approaches the counter and orders:
   - 2x Veg Burger (₹120 each)
   - 1x Cold Coffee (₹80)
   Customer requests: "Please make the coffee less sweet."
   Customer pays with Cash (offers a ₹500 note).

2. ADD ITEMS:
   - On the menu grid, locate "Veg Burger". Click "Add" once, then click [+] once. Cart shows 2x Veg Burger = ₹240.
   - Click "Beverages" category pill. Click "Add" on "Cold Coffee". Cart shows 1x Cold Coffee = ₹80.
   - Current Subtotal: ₹320.00.

3. ENTER NOTES:
   - In the "Special instructions" field, type: "Less sweet coffee".

4. REVIEW & PLACE ORDER:
   - Click the orange "Review & Place Order (3 Items)" button.
   - The Review Modal opens. Confirm 2x Veg Burger (₹240), 1x Cold Coffee (₹80), Note: "Less sweet coffee", Grand Total: ₹320.00.
   - Notice upcoming Token preview: "Token #102".
   - Select Payment Method: "Cash".
   - Click "Confirm & Dispatch Order".

5. SUCCESS & KITCHEN DISPATCH:
   - Success chime plays. The Success Modal opens displaying giant "TOKEN #102".
   - Simultaneously in the kitchen, the KDS displays Token #102 under "Received" with a kitchen bell audio alert!

6. COLLECT PAYMENT & MAKE CHANGE:
   - Click "Collect Payment Now".
   - In the Payment Modal, enter Received Amount: "500".
   - The calculator immediately displays: "Change to Return: ₹180.00".
   - Hand ₹180 cash to the customer.
   - Click "Confirm Cash Payment".

7. PRINT RECEIPT & COMPLETE:
   - The Thermal Receipt preview opens.
   - Click "Print Receipt" (or skip).
   - Click "Start Next Order".
   - The cart clears. The workstation is ready for the next customer in less than 2 seconds.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Audio Feedback:
  Every item added triggers sound.playItemAdd() (a crisp, pleasant 1200Hz sine chime). Order confirmation triggers sound.playSuccess() (a triumphant harmonic chime).
- Atomic Order Creation:
  createOrder() in stallDataService executes an atomic transaction:
  1. Generates sequential order number: ORD-YYYYMMDD-XXXX (scoped per stall per day).
  2. Generates sequential pickup token: #101 through #999 (resets daily per stall).
  3. Freezes item snapshots: Copies item.name, item.sellingPrice, item.costPrice (COGS), and item.category into the order record.
  4. Persists the order into window.localStorage under 'fooddesk_orders'.
  5. Dispatches custom DOM event: window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('fooddesk:order-created', { detail: newOrder })).
  6. Dispatches window 'storage' event for cross-tab synchronization.
- KDS & Tracker Synchronization:
  The KDS screen and the public customer order tracking page listen to fooddesk:order-created and immediately add the new token card to their queues without requiring a page reload.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Sold-Out Protection:
  Items marked as "Sold Out" in Menu Management cannot be added to the cart. If staff attempt to click a sold-out item, a warning toast appears: '"[Item Name]" is currently sold out and cannot be ordered.'
- Double-Click Protection:
  The "Confirm & Dispatch Order" button uses an active isSubmittingRef lock to prevent accidental double-clicks from creating duplicate orders.
- Stall Boundary Enforcement:
  If an owner switches active stalls via the top Navbar, any items currently in the cart are automatically cleared to prevent cross-stall food item mixing.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can I apply both a flat rupee discount and a percentage discount at the same time?
A1: No. Disounts are mutually exclusive per order. You can select either a Flat rupee deduction (e.g., ₹50 off) or a Percentage discount (e.g., 10% off), or None.

Q2: Can a customer pay part in cash and part via UPI?
A2: In the POS quick workflow, select either Cash or UPI. If split tender is required, open the order in the Order Management Hub (/orders) and use the Split Tender payment option.

Q3: What happens if the customer leaves without paying?
A3: The order is recorded with paymentStatus: 'pending'. It appears on the KDS so the food can be cooked, but is flagged as unpaid on the Order Management Hub until payment is collected.

Q4: What happens when pickup tokens reach #999?
A4: FoodDesk tokens run from #101 to #999 and cycle back to #101, ensuring tokens remain compact, recognizable 3-digit numbers suitable for calling out at loud food counters.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Menu Management Hub (/menu) is the central product catalog and pricing administration console for the active food stall.

It allows stall owners to curate their complete food and beverage catalog, create menu categories, adjust retail selling prices, record Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) baselines, evaluate live unit gross profit margins, immediately toggle item stock availability (In Stock vs. Sold Out) during rush hours, and safely archive discontinued items without breaking past order histories or financial ledgers.

Key capabilities include:
- Dual display modes: Visual Grid Cards View and High-Density Table View.
- Five top KPI cards: Active Items, In Stock, Sold Out, Categories, and Archived.
- Segmented navigation tabs separating "Active Menu Items" from "Archived Items".
- Immediate 1-click Availability Toggle: Instantly marks an item as "Sold Out" or "In Stock", synchronizing across POS and public customer menus in real time.
- Integrated Menu Item Creation & Editing Modal with live unit profit and margin calculation.
- Category Management Modal supporting dynamic category creation, renaming, and item association tracking.
- Non-Destructive Soft Archiving: Safely removes discontinued items from the active POS catalog while 100% preserving historical sales, receipts, and P&L snapshots.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct URL: http://localhost:5173/menu
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "Menu Management" (the fifth item with the UtensilsCrossed icon) in the stall navigation sidebar.


3. SCREEN LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Menu Management page is organized into four distinct sections:

1. Top Workstation Bar:
   Active stall badge, workspace title ("Food Menu & Category Management"), Category Manager button, and the primary "Add New Item" button.

2. Five Summary KPI Cards:
   A responsive five-card summary bar displaying Active Items, In Stock, Sold Out, Categories, and Archived item counts.

3. Main Catalog Container Card:
   - Upper Control Bar: Segmented Active vs. Archived tabs, availability filter dropdown, grid vs. table view toggle, real-time search bar, and horizontally scrollable category filter pills.
   - Main Content Canvas: Renders either the responsive card grid or the administrative data table.

4. Modals (Conditional):
   - MenuItemModal: Add or edit item form with pricing, COGS, dietary tags, and image upload.
   - CategoryManagementModal: Manage stall menu categories.
   - ArchiveConfirmModal: Soft archive or restore confirmation dialog.

Every component of the Menu Management Hub is detailed below:

A. WORKSTATION TOP BAR:

B. FIVE SUMMARY KPI CARDS:
1. "Active Items":

   - Count of unique categories configured for this stall.

5. "Archived":
   - Count of items where isArchived === true.
   - Subtext: "Preserves past order records" with slate Archive icon.

C. CATALOG CONTROLS & FILTER BAR:
- Active vs. Archived Segmented Tabs:
  - "Active Menu Items (X)": Displays live catalog.

  - Options: "All States", "In Stock Only", "Sold Out Only".

5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Add New Food Item: Click "Add New Item", fill form, and save.
2. Edit Existing Item: Click Pencil icon on any card/row to modify price, cost, or details.
3. 1-Click Availability Toggle: Click "In Stock" to mark as Sold Out; click "Sold Out" to restore.
4. Manage Categories: Click "Categories" to create, rename, or delete categories.

6. Restore Archived Item: Open Archived tab and click "Restore Item" to bring it back.

6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: MARKING AN ITEM "SOLD OUT" DURING A RUSH
1. The kitchen informs the counter that they have run out of hamburger buns.
2. In Menu Management, locate "Classic Veg Burger".
3. Notice its green button reading "In Stock".
4. Click "In Stock".
5. The button immediately turns yellow reading "Sold Out", and an amber toast appears: '"Classic Veg Burger" marked as Sold Out.'
6. INSTANT RESULT: At the POS workstation and on the public customer menu, the burger is immediately greyed out and blocked from being ordered!

SCENARIO B: ADDING A NEW MENU ITEM WITH PROFIT MARGIN ANALYSIS
1. Click the primary orange "Add New Item" button.
2. In the modal:
   - Name: "Peri Peri Fries".
   - Category: Select "Snacks" (or type a new category).
   - Dietary: Select "Veg".
   - Selling Price: Enter "90".
   - Cost Price (COGS): Enter "35".
3. Look at the Live Economics preview box:
   - Automatically calculates: "Gross Profit: +₹55.00 | Margin: 61%".
4. Description: "Crispy french fries tossed in spicy peri peri seasoning".
5. Image URL: Leave default or paste a photo link.
6. Click "Create Menu Item".
7. A green toast appears: '"Peri Peri Fries" created and added to catalog!'.
8. The item appears immediately in the POS workstation ready for sale.

SCENARIO C: SOFT ARCHIVING A SEASONAL ITEM
1. A seasonal item (e.g., "Mango Shake") is being removed for winter.
2. On the item card, click the Archive icon.
3. The Archive Modal opens confirming that all past orders containing Mango Shake will remain intact in analytics and receipts.
4. Click "Archive Item".
5. The item disappears from the Active tab and is stored safely in the "Archived" tab.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-Time Availability Synchronization:
  When toggleItemAvailability() is called:
  1. Updates item.isAvailable in window.localStorage under 'fooddesk_menu'.
  2. Dispatches custom event: window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('fooddesk:menu_updated')).
  3. Dispatches window 'storage' event.
  4. The POS page and Public Customer Menu listen to this event and re-render their cards instantly without a page refresh.
- Profit & Margin Calculation Logic:
  - Profit (Paise) = toPaise(sellingPrice) - toPaise(costPrice).
  - Margin % = sellingPrice > 0 ? Math.round((profit / sellingPrice) * 100) : 0.
- Soft Archival Integrity:
  Archiving sets item.isArchived = true. It NEVER deletes the item from storage. Historical orders preserve their snapshot cost and price regardless of whether the item is later edited or archived.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Non-Negative Pricing:
  Selling price and cost price must be non-negative numbers. Selling price cannot be zero.
- Soft Archiving vs Hard Deletion:
  FoodDesk strictly prohibits hard-deleting menu items because past orders reference menuItemId. Soft archiving guarantees that past order receipts and historical COGS reports never break.
- Cross-Stall Protection:
  Fast Food Stall (stall-1) can only manage its own 10 items. It cannot view or modify items belonging to Beverages (stall-2) or Desserts (stall-3).


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: If I change the price of an item from ₹100 to ₹120, will past orders change?
A1: Absolutely not. FoodDesk freezes sellingPrice and costPrice inside each order at the moment of checkout. Changing catalog prices only affects future orders.

Q2: Can I restore an archived item?
A2: Yes! Open the "Archived" tab in Menu Management, locate the item, and click "Restore Item". It will return to the active POS catalog immediately.

Q3: Can I add a custom category not in the dropdown?
A3: Yes. In the Add Item modal, simply type your new category name into the category input, or use the "Categories" modal to create it beforehand.

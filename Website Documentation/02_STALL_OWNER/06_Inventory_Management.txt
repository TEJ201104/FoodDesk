1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Inventory and Ingredients Management Hub (/inventory) is the back-of-house raw materials tracking and wastage audit workstation for the active food stall.

It allows stall operators to monitor raw ingredients (such as Burger Buns, Cooking Oil, Paneer, Chicken Breast, Coffee Powder, Milk, Sugar, and Spices), configure minimum reorder thresholds, receive automated low-stock and out-of-stock visual alerts, execute manual stock receipts and adjustments, and record detailed ingredient spoilage, expiry, and prep wastage incidents.

Key capabilities include:
- A prominent Dynamic Low-Stock & Out-of-Stock Alert Banner notifying staff of critical shortages.
- Four top KPI summary cards: Total Items, Optimal Stock, Low Stock Alert, and Out of Stock.
- Three dedicated operational tabs:
  1. Active Ingredients: Real-time stock registry and adjustment console.
  2. Wastage Log: Dedicated audit ledger of ingredient spoilage, damage, and prep losses.
  3. All Stock Movements: Chronological transaction log of every stock intake, reduction, and waste event.
- Predefined commercial units of measure: Kilograms (kg), Grams (g), Litres (l), Millilitres (ml), Pieces (pcs), and Packets.
- Manual Stock Adjustment Modal supporting both additions (restocks) and reductions with mandatory reason codes.
- Dedicated Wastage Recording Modal with reason classifications (Spoilage, Expiry, Prep Loss, Damaged, Other).
- Explicit Prototype Rule: In accordance with PRD Section 9, POS sales do NOT automatically deduct inventory; inventory is tracked manually via periodic physical stock takes, adjustments, and wastage logs.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct URL: http://localhost:5173/inventory
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "Inventory" (the eighth item with the Boxes icon) in the stall navigation sidebar.


3. SCREEN LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Inventory Management screen is organized into five major sections:

1. Page Header & Workstation Actions:
   Title ("Inventory & Ingredients"), subtitle, and three quick action buttons: "Movement Log", "Record Wastage", and the primary "Add Ingredient" button.

2. Dynamic Low-Stock Alert Banner (Conditional):
   An eye-catching amber/rose alert box that appears automatically when any ingredient reaches or drops below its minimum threshold.

3. Four Summary KPI Cards:
   Color-coded cards displaying Total Items (Navy), Optimal Stock (Emerald), Low Stock Alert (Amber), and Out of Stock (Rose).

4. Three Operational Tabs:
   Segmented navigation bar separating "Active Ingredients", "Wastage Log", and "All Stock Movements".

5. Main Data & Audit Views:
   Renders the corresponding table for the active tab with comprehensive filters and action controls.


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every component of the Inventory Management Hub is detailed below:

A. HEADER & WORKSTATION ACTIONS:
- Title & Icon: Boxes icon + "Inventory & Ingredients".
- Subtitle: "Raw materials stock levels, minimum stock thresholds, wastage logs, and low-stock alerts."
- Header Action Buttons:
  1. "Movement Log" (Outline Button): History icon. Opens global stock movement history modal.
  2. "Record Wastage" (Rose Outline Button): Trash2 icon. Opens the wastage incident logger.
  3. "Add Ingredient" (Primary Orange Button): Plus icon. Opens the ingredient creation modal.

B. DYNAMIC LOW-STOCK ALERT BANNER (LowStockAlertBanner.jsx):
- Condition: Visible whenever lowStockItems.length > 0.
- Visual: Amber gradient container with AlertTriangle icon, warning headline, and summary text (e.g., "3 ingredient(s) require urgent restocking!").
- Action Buttons in Banner:
  - "Filter Low Stock Items": Immediately filters the table to display only the low-stock ingredients.
  - "Quick Adjust Stock": Opens the stock adjustment modal for the most critical item.

C. THE FOUR KPI SUMMARY CARDS:
1. TOTAL ITEMS CARD:
   - Header: "TOTAL ITEMS" with Boxes icon in navy box.
   - Primary Number: Total count of tracked ingredients (e.g., "12").
   - Subtext: "Tracked for this stall".
2. OPTIMAL STOCK CARD:
   - Header: "OPTIMAL STOCK" with CheckCircle2 icon in emerald box.
   - Primary Number: Count of items where currentStock > minThreshold (e.g., "8").
   - Subtext: "Above minimum levels".
3. LOW STOCK ALERT CARD:
   - Header: "LOW STOCK ALERT" with AlertTriangle icon in amber box.
   - Primary Number: Count of items where currentStock > 0 AND currentStock <= minThreshold (e.g., "3").
   - Subtext: "At or below threshold".
4. OUT OF STOCK CARD:
   - Header: "OUT OF STOCK" with AlertCircle icon in rose-red box.
   - Primary Number: Count of items where currentStock <= 0 (e.g., "1").
   - Subtext: "Replenish urgently".

D. TAB 1: ACTIVE INGREDIENTS VIEW:
- Filter Toolbar:
  - Search Input: Real-time search across ingredient name, description, and ID.
  - Status Filter Dropdown: "All Stock Statuses", "In Stock (Optimal)", "Low Stock (≤ Threshold)", "Out of Stock (Zero)".
  - Unit Filter Dropdown: "All Units of Measure", "kg", "g", "l", "ml", "pcs", "packets".
  - Results Counter & Reset Filter Button.
- Active Ingredients Table:
  - Column 1: Ingredient Name, description, and internal ID font-mono.
  - Column 2: Current Stock (large bold number + unit, e.g., "2.5 kg"). Color-coded: Rose if zero, Amber if low, Slate if optimal.
  - Column 3: Min Threshold (e.g., "5 kg").
  - Column 4: Stock Status Badge ("In Stock" green, "Low Stock" amber, "Out of Stock" rose).
  - Column 5: Last Updated (Timestamp in Asia/Kolkata timezone).
  - Column 6: Actions:
    - "Adjust" Button (ArrowUpDown icon): Opens StockAdjustmentModal.
    - "Wastage" Button (Trash2 icon): Opens RecordWastageModal pre-filled with this item.
    - "History" Button (History icon): Opens StockHistoryModal filtered for this item.
    - "Edit" Button (Edit2 icon): Opens InventoryItemModal to edit threshold or details.

E. TAB 2: WASTAGE LOG VIEW:
- Dedicated audit table of all wastage incidents:
  - Date / Time (Kolkata timestamp).
  - Ingredient Name.
  - Wasted Quantity (Bold rose text, e.g., "3 kg").
  - Reason Category (Red badge: Spoilage, Expiry, Prep Loss, Damaged, Other).
  - Incident Notes (Detailed explanation).
  - Logged By (User name, e.g., "Owner 1").

F. TAB 3: COMPLETE MOVEMENT LOG VIEW:
- Chronological transaction log of every single stock change:
  - Timestamp.
  - Ingredient Name.
  - Operation Type (Badge: Initial Stock, Addition, Reduction, Wastage).
  - Stock Change (+X or -X with unit).
  - Before → After (e.g., "10 kg → 7 kg").
  - Reason / Notes.
  - Responsible User.

G. MODALS:
- InventoryItemModal: Add/edit ingredient name, unit, initial stock, min threshold, notes.
- StockAdjustmentModal: Adjust stock (Add or Reduce), enter quantity, reason code, notes.
- RecordWastageModal: Record wasted stock with reason category and notes.
- StockHistoryModal: Detailed modal displaying the complete chronological audit timeline for an ingredient.

6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: RECORDING A SUPPLIER RESTOCK SHIPMENT
1. A delivery of 20 kg of Potatoes arrives at the food stall.
2. Open /inventory. Locate "Potatoes" in the Active Ingredients tab.
3. Click the "Adjust" button in the Actions column.
4. The Stock Adjustment Modal opens:
   - Operation Type: Select "Add Stock (+)" (highlighted in emerald green).
   - Quantity to Add: Enter "20".
   - Reason: Select "Supplier Delivery / Purchase".
   - Notes: "Received from Wholesale Vendor, Invoice #4401".
5. Click "Confirm Stock Adjustment".
6. A green toast appears: 'Stock for "Potatoes" adjusted: 4 kg → 24 kg.'
7. The table updates immediately, the status badge updates to "In Stock", and an entry is logged in All Stock Movements.

SCENARIO B: LOGGING INGREDIENT SPOILAGE / WASTAGE
1. Staff discovers that 2 litres of milk have curdled overnight due to a refrigerator issue.
2. In the top header of /inventory, click "Record Wastage".
3. In the Record Wastage Modal:
   - Select Ingredient: Choose "Milk (Cow Milk)".
   - Quantity Wasted: Enter "2". Unit defaults to "l".
   - Wastage Reason: Select "Spoiled / Sour".
   - Incident Notes: "Walk-in cooler tripped breaker overnight; temperature rose to 18C".
4. Click "Record Wastage Incident".
5. An amber toast appears: 'Wastage of 2 l logged for "Milk".'
6. Milk stock automatically decreases by 2 litres. A permanent entry is added to both the Wastage Log tab and the Complete Movement Log.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Unit & Threshold Validation:
  Supported units: 'kg', 'g', 'l', 'ml', 'pcs', 'packets'. Minimum thresholds cannot be negative numbers.
- Automated Status Determination (getInventoryStatus):
  - If currentStock <= 0: status = 'out_of_stock'.
  - If currentStock > 0 AND currentStock <= minThreshold: status = 'low_stock'.
  - If currentStock > minThreshold: status = 'in_stock'.
- Immutable Stock Movement Audit:
  Every stock addition, reduction, initial intake, and wastage event generates an immutable stock movement record containing:
  - id, stallId, ingredientId, ingredientName, type, quantityChanged, previousQuantity, newQuantity, unit, reason, notes, user, timestamp.
- No Automatic POS Sales Deduction Rule:
  In accordance with PRD Section 9, FoodDesk implements manual ingredient tracking. Placing a Burger order at POS does NOT automatically deduct bun or patty inventory. Stock takes and adjustments are performed manually by stall staff.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Stock Reductions Cannot Exceed Current Stock:
  Staff cannot reduce more stock than currently exists unless explicitly authorized (preventing negative inventory balances).
- Strict Stall Isolation:
  Fast Food Stall (stall-1) ingredients (buns, patties, cheese) are completely isolated from Beverages (stall-2) ingredients (tea leaves, coffee beans, milk).
- Reason Code Enforcement:
  Wastage logging strictly enforces selection of a valid category and a descriptive explanation to prevent unrecorded shrinkage.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why doesn't selling a Burger at POS automatically deduct 1 Bun and 1 Patty?
A1: Fast-food stalls and food courts frequently prepare batch items with variable prep yields, recipe customizations, and ingredient substitutions. Automatic deduction without complex ERP recipe bombs often causes inventory drifts. FoodDesk uses a reliable manual periodic adjustment and wastage model per PRD Section 9.

Q2: What is the difference between a Stock Reduction and Wastage?
A2: A Stock Reduction under "Adjust" is used for normal stock reconciliation (e.g., recipe prep, portioning, or physical count correction). "Record Wastage" is specifically for unsellable loss (spoilage, burning, dropping, or expiry) and feeds the dedicated Wastage Audit Log.

Q3: Can I change the unit of an ingredient after creating it?
A3: To maintain mathematical integrity across historical movements, the unit of measure (e.g., kg) should be set correctly on creation. You can edit the description and threshold at any time.

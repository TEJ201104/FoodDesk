1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Expense Management Hub (/expenses) is the dedicated operational expenditure ledger and cost accounting workstation for the active food stall.

In commercial food stalls, business owners frequently pay for daily operational costs (such as wholesale raw vegetables, commercial LPG gas cylinder refills, electricity charges, staff daily wages, paper packaging bags, transportation, and equipment repairs) in cash out of the register till. Without strict logging, these out-of-pocket expenses disappear into thin air, making it impossible to determine whether the stall is operating at a true profit or a loss.

The Expense Management module provides a structured, compliant bookkeeping system that tracks every rupee spent, categorizes expenses across standardized classifications, displays visual breakdown charts, preserves full versioned edit audit trails, and feeds directly into the stall's live P&L engine.

Key capabilities include:
- Eight standardized commercial expense categories: Raw Materials, Gas, Electricity, Salaries, Packaging, Transportation, Maintenance, and Other.
- Three top summary KPI cards: Today's Expenses, This Month's Expenses, and Top Expense Category (anchored strictly to Asia/Kolkata date boundaries).
- Interactive Expense Category Breakdown Chart: A visual percentage distribution bar chart illustrating where money is being spent.
- Multi-criteria filtering: Real-time search across descriptions and IDs, category dropdown, and flexible date range presets (All Dates, Today Only, This Month, and Custom Date Range).
- Detailed Expense Registry Table: Columns for Date, Category badge, Description, Amount (₹), Recorded By, and action controls.
- Versioned Audit Trail Modal: Preserves an immutable history of every revision, showing who edited what, when, and previous values.
- Non-Destructive Deletion Modal: Deleting an expense requires a mandatory reason and archives the record rather than hard-deleting it.

3. SCREEN LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Expense Management screen is organized into five distinct vertical sections:

1. Page Header & Primary Action:
   Title ("Expense Management"), operational subtitle, and the primary "Record Expense" button.

2. Three Summary KPI Cards:
   - Card 1: Today's Expenses (Brand orange accent bar).
   - Card 2: This Month's Expenses (Amber accent bar).
   - Card 3: Top Expense Category (Navy accent bar).

3. Expense Category Breakdown Chart Card:
   A visual distribution card showing total spending and a multi-segmented colored progress bar mapping the proportion of each expense category.

4. Filter & Search Toolbar:
   A consolidated card containing search input, category dropdown, date preset dropdown, custom date inputs (if active), records counter, and a reset filters button.

5. Expense Registry Data Table:
   The main ledger table displaying formatted date, colored category badge, description, rupee amount, recorder audit pill, and action buttons.


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every UI element, button, calculation, and modal is detailed below:

A. HEADER & WORKSTATION ACTION:
- Title & Icon: Wallet icon in brand orange + "Expense Management".
- Subtitle: "Track stall operating costs: raw materials, gas, electricity, salaries, packaging, and maintenance."
- "Record Expense" Button (Primary):
  - Icon: Plus.
  - Action: Opens ExpenseModal in creation mode.

B. THREE SUMMARY KPI CARDS:
1. TODAY'S EXPENSES:
   - Header: "TODAY'S EXPENSES" with Calendar icon in brand orange box.
   - Primary Amount: Bold currency value (e.g., "₹850.00").
   - Calculation: Sum of expense amounts where expense date matches today's date in Asia/Kolkata timezone.
   - Subtext: "Business date: [Today's Formatted Date]".
2. THIS MONTH'S EXPENSES:
   - Header: "THIS MONTH'S EXPENSES" with TrendingDown icon in amber box.
   - Primary Amount: Bold currency value (e.g., "₹18,450.00").
   - Calculation: Sum of expense amounts where expense date starts with the current year-month (YYYY-MM).
   - Subtext: "Active operational spending in current calendar month".
3. TOP EXPENSE CATEGORY:
   - Header: "TOP EXPENSE CATEGORY" with Tag icon in navy box.
   - Primary Text: Name of the category with the highest cumulative spending (e.g., "Raw Materials").
   - Calculation: Category with max(sum(amount)) across active expenses.
   - Subtext: Displays total amount spent in that category (e.g., "₹9,200.00 recorded").

C. EXPENSE BREAKDOWN CHART (ExpenseBreakdownChart.jsx):
- Header: "Expense Breakdown by Category" with total records badge.
- Subtitle: "Distribution of operational costs across predefined categories".
- Visualization:
  - Multi-segmented horizontal progress bar with distinct color coding for each category:
    - Raw Materials (Amber)
    - Gas (Brand Orange)
    - Electricity (Sky Blue)
    - Salaries (Emerald Green)
    - Packaging (Cyan)
    - Transportation (Slate)
    - Maintenance (Rose Red)
    - Other (Neutral Gray)
  - Legend Grid: Displays each category name, colored dot, total rupee amount, and percentage share of total spending (e.g., "Raw Materials: ₹9,200 (50%)").

D. FILTER TOOLBAR:
- Search Input: Real-time search across description, category, and ID.
- Category Selector: Dropdown listing "All Categories" + all 8 predefined categories.
- Date Range Presets:
  - "All Dates": Shows all historical expense entries.
  - "Today Only": Filters for expenses matching current Kolkata date.
  - "This Month": Filters for current calendar month.
  - "Custom Date Range": Reveals two date pickers: "Start Date" and "End Date".
- Results Counter: "Showing X of Y".
- "Reset Filters" Button: Appears when any filter is active.

E. EXPENSE REGISTRY TABLE:
- Column 1: Date (Formatted date, e.g., "16 Nov 2026" + YYYY-MM-DD font-mono).
- Column 2: Category (Color-coded Badge).
- Column 3: Description (Bold description text + expense ID font-mono).
- Column 4: Amount (₹) (Right-aligned bold currency amount, e.g., "₹650.00").
- Column 5: Recorded By / Log:
  - User name who created the entry (e.g., "Owner 1").
  - Version pill: History icon + "X version(s)" or "X revision(s)".
- Column 6: Actions:
  - "View Audit Trail" Button (History icon): Opens ExpenseHistoryModal.
  - "Edit Expense" Button (Edit2 icon): Opens ExpenseModal in edit mode.
  - "Delete Expense" Button (Trash2 icon): Opens ExpenseDeleteModal.

F. MODALS:
1. ADD / EDIT EXPENSE MODAL (ExpenseModal.jsx):
   - Fields:
     1. Date (Date picker, defaults to today).
     2. Category Dropdown (Raw Materials, Gas, Electricity, Salaries, Packaging, Transportation, Maintenance, Other).
     3. Amount (₹) (Numeric input with Indian Rupee prefix).
     4. Description (Text input, e.g., "LPG Commercial Cylinder Refill").
     5. Recorded By / Notes (Staff name or invoice reference).
   - Actions: "Cancel" and "Save Expense Record".
2. AUDIT TRAIL MODAL (ExpenseHistoryModal.jsx):
   - Header: Expense title and ID.
   - Timeline: Chronological list of every version:
     - Version 1 (Creation): Timestamp, author, original amount, original category.
     - Version 2+ (Revisions): Timestamp, author, previous values, new values, modification notes.
3. DELETE CONFIRMATION MODAL (ExpenseDeleteModal.jsx):
   - Header: Rose-red warning dialog.
   - Summary: Displays expense category, amount, and description.
   - Mandatory Reason Input: Staff must enter why the expense is being removed (e.g., "Duplicate entry", "Entered wrong stall").
   - Action: "Confirm Soft Deletion".

6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: LOGGING A CASH PAYMENT FOR A GAS CYLINDER
1. The LPG delivery van arrives at the stall. Staff pays ₹1,200 in cash from the register till.
2. Open /expenses on the stall workstation.
3. Click the orange "Record Expense" button.
4. In the modal:
   - Date: Leave as today's date (defaults to current date).
   - Category: Select "Gas" from the dropdown.
   - Amount: Enter "1200".
   - Description: Enter "Commercial 19kg LPG Cylinder refill".
   - Recorded By: "Owner 1 (Invoice #GAS-991)".
5. Click "Save Expense Record".
6. A green toast appears: 'Expense of ₹1,200.00 recorded under Gas.'
7. The table updates immediately, Today's Expenses KPI increases by ₹1,200, and the Gas segment appears in the Expense Breakdown Chart!

SCENARIO B: EDITING AN INCORRECT AMOUNT AND VIEWING THE AUDIT TRAIL
1. Staff realizes they entered ₹1,200 instead of the actual bill amount of ₹1,150.
2. On the LPG expense row, click the Edit icon (Pencil).
3. Change Amount from "1200" to "1150".
4. Click "Save Expense Record".
5. Notice that the version pill updates from "1 version" to "2 revisions".
6. Click the History icon on that row:
   - The Audit Trail Modal opens, showing:
     - Version 1: Created by Owner 1 with amount ₹1,200.00.
     - Version 2: Updated by Owner 1 with amount ₹1,150.00.
   - Complete accountability is preserved!

SCENARIO C: DELETING AN ACCIDENTAL DUPLICATE ENTRY
1. Staff accidentally entered the same vegetable invoice twice.
2. Click the Trash icon on the duplicate entry.
3. The Delete Modal opens.
4. In the "Reason for Deletion" field, type: "Accidental duplicate entry of morning vegetable bill".
5. Click "Confirm Soft Deletion".
6. The record is removed from the active table and excluded from P&L calculations, while its audit record is archived.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-Time Integration with Net Profit:
  Expenses recorded in this module feed directly into:
  1. Stall Dashboard (/dashboard): Today's expenses are subtracted to compute Estimated Net Profit Today.
  2. Financial Analytics (/analytics): Total period expenses are subtracted from Gross Profit to compute True Net Profit (Net Profit = Gross Profit - Total Expenses).
- Versioned Audit Logging (stallDataService.saveExpense):
  When an existing expense is modified:
  1. The existing record is cloned and pushed into expense.auditTrail array with an ISO timestamp and the editing user's identity.
  2. The main record fields are updated.
  3. Window 'storage' and custom events are dispatched.
- Non-Destructive Soft Deletion (stallDataService.deleteExpense):
  Sets expense.isDeleted = true, records deletionReason, and archives the entry. Deleted expenses are strictly excluded from P&L calculations.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Positive Amount Enforcement:
  Expense amounts must be positive numbers greater than zero. Negative expenses or zero-rupee expenses are rejected with an error.
- Mandatory Reason for Deletion:
  To prevent unauthorized tampering, clicking Delete requires entering a non-empty explanation before the confirmation button is enabled.
- Cross-Stall Segregation:
  Fast Food Stall (stall-1) expenses are completely segregated from Beverages (stall-2) and Desserts (stall-3). Each stall maintains its own private expenditure ledger.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can I add a custom expense category like "Music License"?
A1: FoodDesk uses 8 standardized categories (Raw Materials, Gas, Electricity, Salaries, Packaging, Transportation, Maintenance, Other) to guarantee clean, consistent tax and financial reporting. For specialized costs, select "Other" and describe the specific expense in the description field.

Q2: Does recording an expense reduce the cash balance displayed in POS?
A2: Yes! When viewing the Financial Analytics P&L, expenses directly reduce the stall's net realized profit for the period.

Q3: Can cashiers delete expenses entered by the stall owner?
A3: Staff must provide a mandatory deletion reason. In a multi-user production environment, permission to delete expenses can be restricted to the owner role.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Financial Analytics Hub (/analytics) is the central business intelligence, profit/loss (P&L) calculation, and executive reporting engine of FoodDesk.

In high-volume commercial food stalls, business owners frequently mistake cash flow (total rupees collected at the register) for true profitability. High sales numbers can mask devastating losses if ingredient costs (COGS) are high, excessive discounts are given, or operating expenses (gas, electricity, labor, packaging) eat up the remaining margin.

The Financial Analytics module implements the authoritative P&L calculation formulas defined in PRD Section 11 and the Frontend Implementation Plan. It provides deep, transparent, real-time insight into the stall's actual financial health, calculating Gross Sales, Discounts, Refunds, Net Realized Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Profit, Operating Expenses, and True Net Profit across customizable date boundaries in the Asia/Kolkata timezone.

Key capabilities include:
- A multi-preset Date Range Filter Toolbar: Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, This Year, and Custom Date Range.
- Six core Financial KPI Cards: Gross Sales, Net Revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Profit, Total Expenses, and True Net Profit.
- Recharts Continuous Sales & Profitability Area Chart: A smooth multi-series timeline visualization mapping daily sales, COGS, and profit trends.
- Payment Methods Breakdown Pie Chart: Interactive distribution chart of tender modes (Cash, Dynamic UPI, Static QR, Other).
- Top 5 Best-Selling & Least-Selling Items Bar Chart: Ranked sales analysis by volume and revenue contribution.
- Automated CSV Export Engine: Downloads formatted spreadsheet reports for accountant review.
- Printable A4 Financial Report: Browser-native print modal formatted for executive review.

4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every KPI, calculation formula, chart, and export feature is detailed below:

A. DATE RANGE TOOLBAR (DateRangeFilterToolbar.jsx):
- Date Presets:
  - "Today": Asia/Kolkata current date.
  - "Yesterday": Previous calendar day.
  - "This Week": Current week starting Monday.
  - "This Month": First day of current month to current date (Default).
  - "This Year": January 1st of current year to current date.
  - "Custom Range": Activates start and end date pickers.
- Active Date Meta Label: Displays the formatted human-readable period (e.g., "01 Nov 2026 to 16 Nov 2026").
- Action Buttons:
  - "Export CSV" (Button with Download icon): Generates and downloads a .csv spreadsheet report.
  - "Print Report" (Button with Printer icon): Opens the PrintableFinancialReport modal.
  - Refresh (RotateCcw icon): Re-executes the calculation engine.

B. THE SIX CORE FINANCIAL KPI CARDS (FinancialKpiGrid.jsx):
1. GROSS SALES CARD:
   - Header: "GROSS SALES" with emerald TrendingUp icon.
   - Primary Amount: Sum of all item selling prices * quantities before discounts for eligible paid orders.
   - Formula: Sum of orderSubtotal across realized orders.
   - Subtext: "Total orders placed: [X] • Unpaid/pending excluded".
2. NET REVENUE CARD:
   - Header: "NET REVENUE" with sky-blue CreditCard icon.
   - Primary Amount: Realized money collected from customers.
   - Formula: Gross Sales - Total Discounts Applied - Total Refunds Processed.
   - Subtext: "Discounts: -₹X • Refunds: -₹Y".
3. COST OF GOODS SOLD (COGS) CARD:
   - Header: "COST OF GOODS SOLD (COGS)" with amber Boxes icon.
   - Primary Amount: Direct material cost of food items sold.
   - Formula: Sum of (item.frozenCostPrice * item.quantity) across paid orders.
   - Subtext: "Frozen cost snapshots at checkout time".
4. GROSS PROFIT CARD:
   - Header: "GROSS PROFIT" with emerald CheckCircle2 icon.
   - Primary Amount: Profit generated purely from food sales before operating expenses.
   - Formula: Net Revenue - COGS.
   - Subtext: "Gross Margin: [X]%" where Gross Margin = (Gross Profit / Net Revenue) * 100.
5. TOTAL EXPENSES CARD:
   - Header: "TOTAL OPERATING EXPENSES" with rose-red Wallet icon.
   - Primary Amount: Cumulative operating costs logged in Expense Management.
   - Formula: Sum of active non-deleted expense amounts in period.
   - Subtext: "Operating costs: gas, salaries, utilities, etc.".
6. TRUE NET PROFIT CARD:
   - Header: "TRUE NET PROFIT" with purple Receipt icon.
   - Primary Amount: Final realized bottom-line profit (or loss).
   - Formula: Gross Profit - Total Operating Expenses.
   - Visual State: Renders in bold emerald green if positive; renders in bold rose red if negative (Net Loss).
   - Subtext: "Net Margin: [X]%" where Net Margin = (Net Profit / Net Revenue) * 100.

7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Integer Paise Precision Architecture:
  To eliminate floating-point discrepancies, analyticsService converts every rupee value into integer paise (toPaise(val) = Math.round(val * 100)) before performing arithmetic, and converts back via toRupees(paise) for display.
- Immutable Snapshot COGS Rule:
  Direct cost of goods sold is computed using the item's costPrice frozen inside the order at checkout time (item.costPrice). If the stall owner later updates the item cost in Menu Management, historical COGS remains 100% stable and unchanged.
- Realized Revenue Filter:
  Unpaid orders, pending orders, and cancelled orders with no payment are strictly excluded from Gross Sales and Net Revenue.
- Date Boundary Clamping:
  All date filtering compares ISO date strings against Asia/Kolkata boundary strings (getKolkataDateOnlyString()).

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: What is the difference between Gross Profit and Net Profit?
A1: Gross Profit is what remains after subtracting the raw food material cost (COGS) from your food sales. Net Profit is what you actually take home after subtracting both food costs AND all stall operating expenses (gas, electricity, rent, labor, packaging).

Q2: Why does an unpaid order not show up in Gross Sales?
A2: Because an unpaid order represents uncollected revenue. Commercial accounting requires that only realized, collected revenue is recognized in the P&L statement.

Q3: Can I open the CSV file in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets?
A3: Yes. The exported CSV file uses standard comma-delimited UTF-8 formatting and opens seamlessly in Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice, or Apple Numbers.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Support and Issue Management Hub (/support) is the internal help desk communication portal for the active food stall.

In commercial food stall operations, technical defects, billing disputes, thermal printer wrapping issues, or POS glitches cannot wait days for resolution. Stall operators need a direct, structured line of communication with the platform Super Administrator to report defects, submit operational questions, track ticket progress, and verify resolutions.

The Support module provides a full-featured customer support ticketing system:
- Report New Issue Modal: Submit issues directly to Super Admin with title, category, priority, and rich description.
- Five Standardized Ticket Statuses: Submitted, Under Review, In Progress, Resolved, and Reopened.
- Threaded Conversation Drawer: Real-time messaging view between the stall owner and Super Admin with role badges and timestamps.
- Official Resolution Notes: Prominent verification banner displayed when a Super Admin marks an issue as Resolved.
- Reopen Ticket Safeguard: If an issue is marked Resolved but the problem persists, stall staff can reopen the ticket with a mandatory explanation.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: FILING A HARDWARE TICKET WITH THE SUPER ADMIN
1. The stall's thermal receipt printer is cutting off the right margin on 58mm paper.
2. In the sidebar, click "Report an Issue" (/support).
3. Click the orange "Report New Issue" button.
4. Fill out the form:
   - Issue Title: "Thermal printer 58mm text margin cut off on right side".
   - Category: Select "Hardware / Printer".
   - Priority: Select "High".
   - Detailed Description: "When printing receipts on our 58mm thermal USB printer, the rightmost 4 characters of item names are clipped off the edge of the paper."
5. Review the Context Safeguard box: Confirm it lists your active stall (e.g., Fast Food Stall stall-1).
6. Click "Submit Ticket".
7. A green toast appears: "Support ticket #TKT-XXX submitted to Super Admin."
8. The ticket appears in your list with status "Submitted".

SCENARIO B: REVIEWING A RESOLUTION & REOPENING IF UNRESOLVED
1. When the Super Admin investigates and resolves your ticket, status changes to "Resolved".
2. Locate the ticket in the list. Notice the green "Resolved" badge.
3. Click "View Thread".
4. Read the green Resolution Note Banner:
   - Admin writes: "Updated thermal receipt CSS printable stylesheet to set width to 48mm with auto-wrap padding."
5. Test the printer at the counter: Suppose the margin is still clipped.
6. In the modal footer, click the red "Reopen Issue" button.
7. An urgent input box appears: "Reopen Ticket: Mandatory Follow-Up Comment".
8. Type your explanation: "Tested on receipt #ORD-20261116-0004. Text is still clipping on 58mm rolls."
9. Click "Submit & Reopen Ticket".
10. Ticket status immediately updates to "Reopened" (Red badge), and an alert is sent back to the Super Admin console.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ticket Creation Routine (adminService.createTicket):
  1. Generates sequential ticket ID: TKT-XXX.
  2. Associates ticket with currentStall.id, currentStall.name, user.id, and user.name.
  3. Sets initial status to 'Submitted'.
  4. Appends initial description as Message 1 in the messages array.
  5. Records timestamp in ISO 8601 format.
  6. Persists to localStorage under 'fooddesk_tickets'.
- Reopen Routine (adminService.reopenTicket):
  1. Validates that reopen comment is non-empty.
  2. Updates ticket.status to 'Reopened'.
  3. Appends a new message authored by the owner explaining the reopening.
  4. Appends an entry to ticket.history log: { status: 'Reopened', note: comment, timestamp }.
- Strict Tenancy Filter:
  getTickets() automatically filters by stallId: currentStall.id. Stall 1 cannot view tickets submitted by Stall 2 or Stall 3.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Non-Empty Fields Mandatory:
  Issue Title and Detailed Description cannot be empty or whitespace-only.
- Mandatory Reopen Justification:
  Staff cannot simply click "Reopen" without explanation; a non-empty comment explaining why the issue persists is strictly required.
- Stall Boundary Segregation:
  Support tickets are bound to both the stall owner account AND the active physical stall ID.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Who receives my support tickets?
A1: All tickets are delivered directly to the platform Super Administrator's centralized Issue Management Console (/admin/tickets).

Q2: Can I delete a support ticket?
A2: No. Support tickets are preserved as a permanent historical compliance record to ensure platform accountability and track recurring operational defects.

Q3: Can I attach a screenshot to a ticket?
A3: In the demonstration build, paste screenshot links or image URLs into the description field. In production releases, an S3 file uploader allows direct image attachments.

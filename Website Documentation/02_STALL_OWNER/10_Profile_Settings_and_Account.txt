1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Stall Profile & Workspace Configuration Hub (/settings) is the central administrative profile and operating parameters console for the active food stall.

It allows stall owners to inspect their official business metadata, physical counter location, daily operating hours, payment gateway and UPI configuration, and client-side multi-tenant isolation status.

Key capabilities include:
- Visual display of official business identity: Stall Name, Unique Stall ID, URL slug, food category, and public description.
- Physical counter address: Street, landmark, city, postal pincode, and authorized contact telephone.
- Daily operating schedule: Service opening time, closing time, and weekly active schedule.
- Payment Gateway & UPI settings: Stall UPI VPA, Payee Name, Cash payment toggle, Static QR toggle, and Dynamic Gateway QR toggle.
- Workspace Guard Status: Visual confirmation that StallGuard runtime protection and client-side tamper recovery are active.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct URL: http://localhost:5173/settings
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "Stall Settings" (the tenth item with the Settings icon) in the stall navigation sidebar.


3. SCREEN LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Stall Settings page uses a clean two-column desktop layout (1 column on mobile, 3 columns on large screens with a 2-to-1 split):

1. Page Header:
   Title ("Stall Profile & Workspace Configuration"), subtitle, and an emerald green status badge ("Module 3 Scoped Profile").

2. Left Column (2 cols on lg):
   - Stall Identity Card: Primary business name, ID, slug, category, public description, address, and contact number.
   - Operating Timings Card: Daily service hours (opening/closing times) and weekly operating schedule.

3. Right Column (1 col on lg):
   - Payment Gateway & UPI Card: Configured UPI VPA address, payee name, and accepted tender method badges (Cash, Static QR, Dynamic QR).
   - Workspace Guard Card: Active StallGuard status confirmation and multi-tenant isolation audit details.

5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Review Stall Configuration: Verify that counter addresses, operating hours, and UPI VPAs are accurate.
2. Verify Payment Settings: Check which payment modes (Cash, Static QR, Dynamic QR) are active for the counter POS.
3. Check Multi-Tenant Security: Review the Workspace Guard card to confirm data isolation integrity.
4. Switch Workspaces (Multi-Stall Owners): Use the top Navbar to switch stalls and view another stall's settings.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO: VERIFYING COUNTER UPI CONFIGURATION BEFORE OPENING
1. Open /settings from the stall navigation sidebar.
2. In the right-hand column, review the "Payment Gateway & UPI" card.
3. Confirm that the "Stall UPI VPA" displays your correct merchant handle (e.g., "fastfood@upi").
4. Confirm that the "Payee" matches your registered business bank account.
5. Confirm that Cash, Static UPI QR, and Dynamic Payment QR all display green "Enabled" badges.
6. Your counter workstation is ready to accept all customer tender methods!


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dynamic StallContext Binding:
  StallSettingsPage reads currentStall directly from useStall(). When a multi-stall owner switches between stall-1 (Fast Food) and stall-2 (Beverages), the settings page updates instantly to display the corresponding address, operating hours, and UPI VPA without reloading.
- Storage Abstraction:
  Data is backed by storageService.getStallById(stallId) from the persistent 'fooddesk_stalls' collection.

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Where does the UPI VPA come from?
A1: The UPI VPA is configured during stall onboarding. It is used by the POS payment modal to generate scannable Bharat QR / UPI payment links for customer smartphones.

Q2: What is the URL slug used for?
A2: The URL slug (e.g., fast-food-stall) is the public identifier used for customer-facing web links, such as the Public Digital Menu (/menu/fast-food-stall) and Public Order Tracker (/track/fast-food-stall).

Q3: Can a stall owner operate outside their posted operating hours?
A3: Yes. Operating hours are displayed on the public menu for customer information, but do not block staff from entering counter orders at the POS if the kitchen chooses to open early or stay open late.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Super Admin Platform Dashboard (/admin/dashboard) is the centralized global governance cockpit for the FoodDesk platform.

Unlike stall owners who focus on front-of-house counter POS terminals, kitchen display orders, and daily stall profitability, the Super Administrator oversees the entire multi-tenant network of commercial food stalls. The Platform Dashboard aggregates system-wide commercial activity, monitors stall compliance, tracks owner account authorizations, oversees total platform Gross Merchandising Volume (GMV), and provides immediate oversight of unresolved support tickets.

Key capabilities include:
- Four Platform-Wide Primary KPI Cards: Platform Gross Sales (GMV), Stall Network status, Registered Stall Owners, and Support & Issue Desk queue.
- Quick Governance Navigation Banner providing one-click shortcuts to Stall Approvals, Owner Registry, Central Issue Desk, and Platform Settings.
- Stall Directory & Pipeline Status Widget: Real-time preview of all platform stalls and their current lifecycle states (Active, Pending Approval, Suspended, Rejected).
- Unresolved Support Requests Widget: High-visibility queue of open tickets requiring immediate administrator review or response.
- Platform Audit & System Activity Feed: Chronological log of system actions, stall approvals, suspensions, and support resolutions.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct URL: http://localhost:5173/admin/dashboard
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "Admin Dashboard" (the first item with the Shield icon) in the Super Admin sidebar.
- Post-Login Redirect: Super Admins logging in via the "Super Admin Portal" tab at /login are automatically forwarded to this screen.
- Role Guard Protection: Unauthenticated users are redirected to /login; Stall Owners attempting to access this URL are intercepted and redirected to /dashboard.

7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Platform Metrics Calculation (adminService.getPlatformMetrics):
  1. Queries all orders across all stalls from storageService.getOrders().
  2. Aggregates platformGrossSales (sum of subtotals of all paid orders across all stalls).
  3. Aggregates platformNetRevenue (Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds).
  4. Counts stalls by status: totalStalls, activeStallsCount, pendingStallsCount, suspendedStallsCount.
  5. Counts owner accounts: totalOwnersCount.
  6. Counts tickets: totalTicketsCount, unresolvedTicketsCount, inProgressTicketsCount, underReviewTicketsCount, resolvedTicketsCount.
- Audit Log Polling (adminService.getActivityLog):
  Reads the chronological audit log array maintained in localStorage under 'fooddesk_admin_activity'.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Super Admin Role Exclusivity:
  Only authenticated accounts with role: 'super_admin' can access this screen. Stall owners are strictly blocked by RoleGuard.
- Global Scope:
  Unlike the Stall Dashboard which is strictly isolated to a single stall, the Platform Dashboard intentionally aggregates data across ALL stalls for governance purposes.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can a Super Admin take orders or ring up customers from this dashboard?
A1: No. Super Admins do not operate counter cash registers or kitchen displays. FoodDesk enforces strict separation of concerns: stall staff manage food preparation and POS sales, while Super Admins govern platform compliance.

Q2: What is Platform Gross Merchandising Volume (GMV)?
A2: GMV represents the total cumulative value of all food and beverage sales completed across every stall operating on the FoodDesk platform.

Q3: How are system activities recorded in the activity feed?
A3: Whenever an administrative action is performed (approving a stall, rejecting an application, suspending a stall, replying to a ticket, or resetting demo data), adminService automatically appends a timestamped audit record to the platform activity ledger.

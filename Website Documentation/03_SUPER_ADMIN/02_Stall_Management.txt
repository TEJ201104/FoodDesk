1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Stall Management Console (/admin/stalls) is the central stall onboarding, regulatory compliance, and lifecycle administration workstation for the platform Super Administrator.

Commercial food courts and marketplace platforms must maintain strict governance over who operates food stalls on the platform. When an entrepreneur applies to open a new food counter, the platform administrator must inspect their business category, assigned owner, physical counter location, contact details, and operating schedule before granting operational access. Furthermore, if a stall violates food safety regulations, fails to pay market fees, or ceases operations, the administrator must have the power to suspend or reactivate stalls with full audit trails.

Key capabilities include:
- A comprehensive Stall Directory Table listing all registered and pending food stalls.
- Multi-criteria filtering: Search across stall names, IDs, and cities; filter by Lifecycle Status (Active, Pending Approval, Suspended, Rejected); filter by Assigned Owner.
- 1-Click Stall Approval: Approve pending applications, immediately provisioning operational POS access.
- Rejection Workflow with Mandatory Feedback: Reject non-compliant applications with mandatory explanation notes delivered to the applicant.
- Suspension Workflow with Reason Logging: Temporarily lock operational access for non-compliant stalls.
- Reactivation Workflow: Restore previously suspended stalls back to full active status.
- New Stall Application Modal: Register a new food stall on behalf of an owner.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct URL: http://localhost:5173/admin/stalls
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "Stall Approvals" (the second item with the Store icon) in the Super Admin sidebar.
- Platform Dashboard Shortcut: Click "Stall Approvals" or "View All Stalls" on the Admin Dashboard (/admin/dashboard).

6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: ONBOARDING & APPROVING A NEW STALL APPLICATION
1. Click the orange "New Stall Application" button.
2. Fill out the application form:
   - Stall Business Name: "South Indian Tiffin Corner".
   - Category: "South Indian & Breakfast".
   - Assigned Owner: Select "Owner 1".
   - Description: "Authentic idli, dosa, vada, and filter coffee."
   - Contact Number: "+91 98765 44444".
   - Address: "Kiosk #18, Food Court, Indore — 452001".
3. Click "Submit Stall Application".
4. The stall appears at the top of the table with an amber "Pending Approval" badge.
5. Review the details by clicking the Eye icon.
6. Click the green "Approve" button on the row.
7. A green toast appears: 'Stall "South Indian Tiffin Corner" approved successfully.'
8. The status immediately updates to "Active" (Emerald badge). Owner 1 can now select and operate this stall!

SCENARIO B: SUSPENDING A NON-COMPLIANT STALL
1. Fast Food Stall fails a quarterly food court cleanliness audit.
2. Locate "Fast Food Stall" (stall-1) in the table. Notice its green "Active" badge.
3. Click the red "Suspend" button in the Actions column.
4. The Suspension Reason Modal opens.
5. In the reason field, type: "Failed food court quarterly cleanliness audit. Suspended until deep cleaning reinspection."
6. Click "Confirm Stall Suspension".
7. An amber toast appears: 'Stall "Fast Food Stall" suspended.'
8. The status updates to "Suspended" (Red badge).
9. SYSTEM IMPACT: Fast Food Stall is immediately blocked from logging in or taking POS orders until reinspected and reactivated.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Approval Routine (adminService.approveStall):
  1. Sets stall.status = 'active'.
  2. Updates stall.approvedAt = new Date().toISOString().
  3. Updates stall.approvedBy = adminUser.
  4. Appends an entry to platform activity feed.
  5. Automatically adds stall.id to the assigned owner's authorizedStalls array in initialUsers.
- Suspension Routine (adminService.suspendStall):
  1. Sets stall.status = 'suspended'.
  2. Records suspensionReason and suspendedAt timestamp.
  3. Appends an entry to platform activity feed.
  4. If a cashier attempts to log in to this stall, mockAuthService rejects the login: "Stall '[Stall Name]' is currently suspended."
- Reactivation Routine (adminService.reactivateStall):
  1. Restores stall.status = 'active'.
  2. Clears suspension flags.
  3. Restores full POS and KDS operational access.

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: What happens to active orders if a stall is suspended?
A1: Existing historical orders and financial records are preserved for accounting purposes. However, the counter terminal is locked, preventing staff from creating new POS orders.

Q2: Can an owner operate multiple stalls simultaneously?
A2: Yes! When an administrator approves a new stall assigned to Owner 1, the new stall ID is added to Owner 1's authorizedStalls array. Owner 1 can then switch between all approved stalls via the top Navbar switcher.

Q3: Does rejecting a stall delete it from the database?
A3: No. The application record is preserved with status: 'rejected' and the recorded rejection feedback, maintaining a complete regulatory audit trail.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Stall Details & Governance Drawer is the deep compliance inspection workstation available within the Super Admin Stall Management module.

While the main Stall Directory Table provides a high-level overview of stall names and statuses, platform administrators frequently need to inspect the full, granular legal and technical profile of a stall. This includes reviewing authorized owner contact credentials, full physical counter addresses, official operating schedules, payment gateway configurations (UPI VPA and merchant handles), public URL slugs, and historical governance logs (approvals, rejections, suspensions, and reactivations).

The Stall Details Drawer provides a dedicated, slide-out modal panel that surfaces all raw metadata for any selected food stall, enabling administrators to conduct thorough compliance audits without navigating away from the main directory.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Location: Available inside the Stall Management Console (/admin/stalls).
- Trigger: Click the "Inspect" button (Eye icon) in the Actions column on any stall row in the directory table.


3. SCREEN LAYOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Stall Details view renders as an elevated slide-out drawer or centered modal dialog:

1. Modal Header:
   Stall Name, Unique Stall ID, URL slug, Lifecycle Status Badge (Active, Pending, Suspended, Rejected), and close (X) button.

2. Five Granular Information Sections:
   - Section 1: Business Identity & Description.
   - Section 2: Assigned Owner & Contact Information.
   - Section 3: Physical Counter Location & Address.
   - Section 4: Operating Hours & Weekly Schedule.
   - Section 5: Payment Gateway & Merchant UPI Settings.

3. Historical Governance Log Box:
   Chronological timeline recording when the stall was registered, who approved it, reasons for any past suspensions, and reactivation dates.

4. Modal Footer:
   Action buttons to close the drawer or trigger immediate lifecycle transitions (Approve, Suspend, Reactivate).


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every data field and section within the Stall Details Drawer is detailed below:

A. HEADER SECTION:
- Stall Name & Avatar: Large bold title with stall ID badge (e.g., "Fast Food Stall (stall-1)").
- URL Slug: Monospace public routing slug (e.g., "slug: fast-food-stall").
- Status Badge: High-contrast status indicator (Emerald for Active, Amber for Pending, Red for Suspended/Rejected).
- Close Button: Standard X icon in top-right corner.

B. BUSINESS IDENTITY SECTION:
- Business Category: Full classification text (e.g., "Fast Food & Snacks").
- Detailed Description: Paragraph detailing the stall's menu specialty, food offerings, and customer service approach.
- Public Menu Link: Clickable preview link to the stall's public walk-in menu (/menu/:slug).

C. ASSIGNED OWNER & CONTACT SECTION:
- Owner Name: Full legal name of the registered owner (e.g., "Owner 1").
- Owner ID: Canonical user ID (e.g., "owner_1").
- Registered Email Address: Official account email (e.g., "owner1@gmail.com").
- Telephone Contact: Merchant contact number (e.g., "+91 98765 11111").

D. PHYSICAL COUNTER LOCATION SECTION:
- Kiosk / Counter Number: Street and kiosk identifier (e.g., "Kiosk #14, Food Street").
- Landmark: Market landmark (e.g., "North Gate Plaza").
- City & State: Operating city (e.g., "Indore, MP").
- Postal Pincode: 6-digit postal code (e.g., "452001").

E. OPERATING HOURS & SCHEDULE SECTION:
- Daily Opening Time: e.g., "10:00 AM".
- Daily Closing Time: e.g., "11:00 PM".
- Weekly Days Active: "Open All 7 Days (Mon – Sun)".

F. PAYMENT GATEWAY & MERCHANT SETTINGS:
- Merchant UPI VPA: Virtual Payment Address (e.g., "fastfood@upi").
- Payee Name: Official bank account payee name (e.g., "Fast Food Enterprises").
- Accepted Tender Flags:
  - Cash Allowed: True / False.
  - Static QR Allowed: True / False.
  - Dynamic Gateway QR Allowed: True / False.

G. HISTORICAL GOVERNANCE AUDIT LOG:
- Displays past lifecycle events:
  - Created Date & Submitter.
  - Approval Date & Approving Administrator Name.
  - Suspension Log: Recorded suspension reason and timestamp (if applicable).
  - Rejection Feedback: Recorded rejection notes (if applicable).


5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Inspect Full Metadata: Review all operational and legal parameters of the food stall.
2. Preview Public Menu: Click the public menu link to verify how the stall appears to walk-in customers.
3. Verify Merchant UPI: Confirm that the stall's bank payee name and UPI ID are legitimate.
4. Execute Direct Lifecycle Transition: Approve, suspend, or reactivate the stall directly from the drawer footer.
5. Close Drawer: Click the X button, press Escape, or click outside to dismiss the drawer.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO: CONDUCTING A PRE-APPROVAL COMPLIANCE INSPECTION
1. In the Stall Management directory (/admin/stalls), locate a new application with status "Pending Approval" (e.g., "South Indian Tiffin Corner").
2. Click the Eye icon ("Inspect") in the Actions column.
3. The Stall Details Drawer slides open.
4. REVIEW BUSINESS IDENTITY:
   - Verify that the stall name and category ("South Indian & Breakfast") are appropriate.
   - Check the public description for clarity.
5. REVIEW LOCATION & OWNER:
   - Check the physical kiosk number: "Kiosk #18, Food Court".
   - Confirm that the assigned owner is a verified user ("Owner 1").
   - Check the contact telephone number.
6. REVIEW PAYMENT & OPERATING HOURS:
   - Confirm operating hours: "08:00 AM – 10:00 PM".
   - Confirm UPI VPA is configured.
7. TAKE ACTION:
   - Click "Approve Stall" at the bottom of the drawer.
   - The drawer closes, a green toast confirms approval, and the stall is immediately live!


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Reactive Data Binding:
  The drawer pulls live data directly from the selected stall object managed by adminService. Any updates made in the directory immediately synchronize with the drawer view.
- Non-Destructive Inspection:
  Opening, reviewing, and closing the drawer is a strictly read-only inspection action that incurs zero state mutation unless an action button is explicitly clicked.

- Immediate update of lifecycle status upon approval or suspension.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Confidential Administrative Data:
  The Stall Details Drawer surfaces internal merchant contact numbers, owner IDs, and administrative governance notes that are strictly hidden from public customers and other stall owners.
- Super Admin Only:
  This detailed compliance drawer can only be viewed by authenticated Super Admins.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can a Super Admin edit a stall's address or name directly from this drawer?
A1: In the demonstration prototype, stall metadata is created during onboarding and inspected via this drawer. In production releases, an "Edit Metadata" button enables authorized administrators to modify address and schedule parameters.

Q2: Where do the suspension notes appear if a stall is suspended?
A2: Suspension notes are displayed prominently inside the Historical Governance Audit Log box in this drawer, ensuring future auditors know exactly why the stall was suspended.

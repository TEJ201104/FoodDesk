1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Public Customer Digital Menu Portal (/menu/:stallSlug) is an unauthenticated, customer-facing web portal designed for walk-in food stall patrons.

In commercial food courts, markets, and busy street plazas, physical laminated paper menus get dirty, torn, outdated, and crowd the ordering counter. FoodDesk solves this by generating an instant, zero-maintenance digital menu for every registered stall. Stalls simply print and display a static QR code at their counter or on dining tables. Customers scan the QR code with their own smartphones to instantly view the stall's full live menu without downloading any mobile app or creating an account.

Key capabilities include:
- Dynamic URL slug binding (e.g., /menu/fast-food-stall, /menu/beverages-stall, /menu/desserts-stall).
- High-visibility Stall Brand Header: Displays stall business name, ID, category, public description, counter address, and daily operating hours.
- Real-time Category Navigation Pills: Filter items by category with real-time count badges.
- Instant Keyword Search: Filter dishes by name, ingredients, or dietary preference.
- Dietary Veg / Non-Veg Indicators: Universal green and red dietary badges.
- Live Stock Availability: If an item is marked "Sold Out" by staff at the counter, it immediately updates on customer phones with a prominent yellow "Sold Out" overlay.
- Strict Walk-In Counter Presentation Rule: Per PRD Section 13, this is a read-only digital viewing menu. There is no shopping cart, no online checkout, and no customer self-ordering. A prominent counter notice instructs customers to place orders directly with the stall staff.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Direct Public URLs:
  - Fast Food Stall: http://localhost:5173/menu/fast-food-stall
  - Beverages Stall: http://localhost:5173/menu/beverages-stall
  - Desserts Stall: http://localhost:5173/menu/desserts-stall
- Counter QR Code: Customers scan the printed counter QR code linking directly to the stall's slug URL.
- Application Shortcuts:
  - Login Page Footer: Click "Public Customer Menu".
  - Stall Owner Navbar: Click "Customer Menu" (ExternalLink icon).
  - Stall Owner Sidebar: Click "Digital Menu (Walk-in)".
- Zero Authentication: Requires NO username, password, or login session. Completely public.

7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Slug Parameter Resolution:
  The page reads stallSlug from react-router useParams() and executes stallDataService.getPublicMenu(stallSlug):
  1. Finds the stall matching stall.slug === stallSlug.
  2. If stall does not exist, renders an explicit error card: "Stall Menu Unavailable. No active stall found matching slug: [stallSlug]".
  3. Retrieves active menu items for that stall where item.isArchived !== true.
  4. Strips internal costPrice and profit data before returning to the view.
- Real-Time Synchronization:
  The page subscribes to window.addEventListener('fooddesk:menu_updated', ...) and 'storage'.
  If staff mark an item as "Sold Out" at the counter, the customer's open mobile browser tab updates immediately!

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No Online Cart or Checkout:
  In accordance with PRD Section 13, this is an in-person walk-in viewing portal. Customers cannot add items to a cart or execute online payment from this page; orders are placed with the cashier at the counter.
- Archival Exclusion:
  Archived menu items are automatically excluded from the public menu. Only active catalog items are displayed.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Does a customer have to log in to see the menu?
A1: No! The menu is 100% public. Customers scan the QR code and see the menu immediately without entering an email, phone number, or password.

Q2: What happens if a customer scans the QR code after the stall is closed?
A2: The menu remains accessible so customers can browse offerings for tomorrow, and clearly displays the operating hours (e.g., "10:00 AM – 11:00 PM").

Q3: Can a customer see the menu of other stalls from this link?
A3: No. Each link is strictly bound to that specific stall's slug (e.g., /menu/fast-food-stall). Stalls never cross-contaminate each other's menus.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Complete POS Order Workflow is the primary revenue-generating operational loop in FoodDesk. It traces the complete, uninterrupted lifecycle of a customer order from the moment a patron steps up to the food stall counter to the moment their hot food is cooked, served, and accounted for in the daily P&L.

This workflow coordinates six distinct modules:
1. Authentication (verifying cashier authorization).
2. Counter POS (capturing items, custom notes, and discounts).
3. Payment Processing (collecting cash or verifying dynamic UPI).
4. Thermal Receipt Printing (issuing the printed token ticket).
5. Kitchen Display System (expediting cooking across 4 Kanban stages).
6. Financial Analytics (freezing COGS snapshots and realizing profit).


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Entry Workstation: Counter Point of Sale (POS) at http://localhost:5173/pos
- Prerequisite: An authenticated stall owner session (e.g., Owner 1 with stall-1).


3. SCREEN LAYOUT & STAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The workflow progresses sequentially through six visual screens and modals:
- Stage 1: Front Counter Catalog Grid & Cart Sidebar (/pos).
- Stage 2: POS Order Review Modal.
- Stage 3: Order Success Modal (displaying giant pickup token).
- Stage 4: Payment Processing Modal (Cash calculator or UPI QR).
- Stage 5: Digital Thermal Receipt Modal (58mm/80mm print).
- Stage 6: Kitchen Display Kanban Board (/kds).


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key operational elements involved in this end-to-end workflow:
- Catalog Add Buttons: Increments line item quantities.
- Cart Sidebar Line-Items: Shows item name, unit price, quantity stepper, and total.
- Customer Instructions Textarea: Captures dietary or culinary modifications (e.g., "extra spicy").
- Discount Selector: Segmented control for Flat (₹) or Percentage (%) reductions.
- Review Order Button: Triggers validation checks before placing the order.
- Pickup Token (#101–#999): High-contrast 3-digit identifier used to call out orders.
- Order Number (ORD-YYYYMMDD-XXXX): Unique, sequential tax invoice reference.


5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Add/Remove dishes to/from cart.
2. Apply flat or percentage discounts.
3. Record customer phone number and cooking instructions.
4. Review order totals in pre-confirmation modal.
5. Confirm order creation (assigns token and dispatches to kitchen).
6. Collect tender via Cash (with change calculator) or UPI QR.
7. Print physical thermal receipt for the customer.
8. Advance kitchen status on KDS (Received → Preparing → Ready → Completed).


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: ORDER CAPTURE AT THE COUNTER
1. Cashier stands at POS terminal (/pos). Customer steps up to counter.
2. Cashier clicks items ordered by customer:
   - 2x Classic Veg Burger (₹120 each = ₹240).
   - 1x Masala Fries (₹90).
3. Customer asks for: "No mayo on the burgers".
   - Cashier types "No mayo" into the "Special instructions" field.
4. Customer presents a 10% student discount coupon:
   - Cashier clicks "% Percent" discount button, enters "10".
   - Bill updates in real time: Subtotal ₹330.00, Discount -₹33.00, Grand Total ₹297.00.
5. Cashier clicks "Review & Place Order (3 Items)".

PHASE 2: PRE-CONFIRMATION & CREATION
6. Review Modal opens. Cashier confirms items, notes ("No mayo"), and Grand Total (₹297.00).
7. Notice token preview: "Token #103".
8. Cashier selects Payment Method: "Cash".
9. Cashier clicks "Confirm & Dispatch Order".

PHASE 3: CELEBRATION & TOKEN ASSIGNMENT
10. Success chime sounds. Success Modal appears with giant "TOKEN #103".
11. Cashier calls out: "Token 103!".
12. Simultaneously, in the kitchen, the KDS chimes a bell alert as Token #103 appears in the "Received" column!

PHASE 4: PAYMENT COLLECTION
13. Cashier clicks "Collect Payment Now" in the Success Modal.
14. Customer hands over a ₹500 currency note.
15. Cashier enters "500" into the Tendered Amount field.
16. Calculator immediately displays: "Change to Return: ₹203.00".
17. Cashier hands ₹203 change to customer and clicks "Confirm Cash Payment".

PHASE 5: THERMAL RECEIPT ISSUANCE
18. Thermal Receipt modal opens displaying itemized tax invoice and Token #103.
19. Cashier clicks "Print Receipt". Receipt prints from USB thermal printer.
20. Cashier tears off receipt, hands it to customer, and clicks "Start Next Order".
21. POS cart clears immediately. Ready for next customer in line!

PHASE 6: KITCHEN EXPEDITION & SERVING
22. Kitchen chef reads Token #103 on KDS.
23. Chef taps "Start Preparing" (card moves to Preparing).
24. When burgers are wrapped, chef taps "Mark as Ready" (card moves to Ready).
25. Counter expediter calls: "Token 103 Ready!". Customer presents receipt slip.
26. Expediter hands over food tray and taps "Complete & Serve".
27. Order moves to Completed. Workflow complete!


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- State Transitions:
  orderStatus transitions: received → preparing → ready → completed.
  paymentStatus transitions: pending → paid.
- Accounting & COGS Snapshot:
  When createOrder() executes, each item's costPrice is locked into the order record. At checkout, net revenue (₹297.00) is credited to the stall's realized sales, and snapshot COGS (e.g., ₹130.00) is recognized, yielding a gross profit of ₹167.00.
- Multi-Screen Event Flow:
  POS confirmation dispatches 'fooddesk:order-created', which instantly wakes up KDS and Order History without polling or network delays.

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can an order be modified after it is confirmed?
A1: Once confirmed, an order is locked to protect kitchen integrity. If the customer wants to add another drink, cashiers ring up a quick supplementary ticket.

Q2: What if the customer wants a refund after the order is completed?
A2: Open the Order Management Hub (/orders), click "Details" on the order row, click "Process Refund", enter the refund amount and reason, and confirm.

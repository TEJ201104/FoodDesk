1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Order-to-Kitchen Workflow governs the critical operational bridge between front-of-house counter staff and back-of-house kitchen preparation cooks.

In commercial restaurants and food stalls, communication breakdowns between cashiers and cooks are the primary cause of wrong orders, delayed tickets, and customer complaints. The Order-to-Kitchen Workflow eliminates physical paper tickets entirely. When an order is finalized at the POS counter, it instantly materializes on the Kitchen Display System (KDS) monitor accompanied by an audible bell alert. Kitchen staff advance the order across four standardized Kanban stages, ensuring complete transparency and zero lost orders.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Front-of-House Screen: POS Workstation at http://localhost:5173/pos
- Back-of-House Screen: Kitchen Display System at http://localhost:5173/kds
- Dual-Screen Setup: Typically run on two separate devices (e.g., POS terminal on counter tablet; KDS on kitchen wall-mounted touch monitor).


3. SCREEN LAYOUT & STAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This workflow operates across a 4-column Kanban queue:
- Stage 1: Received Column (New incoming tickets from POS).
- Stage 2: Preparing Column (Active cooking & assembly).
- Stage 3: Ready for Pickup Column (Plated food waiting at counter).
- Stage 4: Completed Column (Served tickets).


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key operational elements involved in this workflow:
- Sequential Pickup Token (#101–#999): High-visibility identifier matching receipt slip.
- Special Customer Notes Banner: High-contrast amber box highlighting modifications (e.g., "Extra spicy, no onion").
- 1-Second Live Elapsed Timer: Shows wait time with color-coded alerts (Green < 5m, Yellow 5-10m, Red > 10m).
- Stage Progression Touch Buttons:
  - "Start Preparing" (Flame icon).
  - "Mark as Ready" (BellRing icon).
  - "Complete & Serve" (CheckCircle2 icon).
- Audio Bell Synthesizer: Zero-latency Web Audio API chime.


5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Place order at POS terminal (dispatches ticket to kitchen).
2. Hear kitchen bell chime when new ticket arrives.
3. Touch "Start Preparing" to signal line cooks have begun cooking.
4. Touch "Mark as Ready" when food is boxed/plated.
5. Call out customer token number.
6. Touch "Complete & Serve" when handing food to customer.
7. Revert accidental stage advance if clicked prematurely.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: CASHIER CREATES ORDER AT POS
- Cashier enters 2x Burger and 1x Fries with note "Well done fries".
- Cashier confirms order. Success screen shows Token #104.

STEP 2: IMMEDIATE ARRIVAL IN KITCHEN (0 MILLISECONDS)
- Across the counter in the kitchen, the KDS monitor chimes a two-tone kitchen bell.
- A new card appears at the top of the "Received" column displaying:
  - Giant Token: "#104".
  - Items: "2x Burger, 1x Fries".
  - Warning Banner: "Well done fries".
  - Timer: "0m 01s ago" (Green).

STEP 3: LINE COOK STARTS PREPARATION
- Line cook drops the fries into the fryer and places patties on the grill.
- Cook touches "Start Preparing" on card #104.
- Card slides into Column 2 ("Preparing"). Wait time continues tracking.

STEP 4: FOOD IS READY FOR CUSTOMER
- Cook boxes the burgers and fries.
- Cook touches "Mark as Ready".
- Completion bell plays. Card slides into Column 3 ("Ready for Pickup").
- Simultaneously, the customer's phone tracker updates to "Ready for Pickup"!

STEP 5: HANDOVER & ARCHIVAL
- Counter expediter calls: "Token 104!".
- Customer presents receipt slip. Expediter verifies token and hands over tray.
- Expediter touches "Complete & Serve" on card #104.
- Card moves to Column 4 ("Completed"). Order queue is cleared.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Event Dispatch Architecture:
  POS order placement fires: window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('fooddesk:order-created', { detail: order })).
  KDS stage progression fires: window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('fooddesk:order-status-updated', { detail: order })).
- Multi-Tab & Cross-Device Sync:
  Changes synchronize across multiple browser tabs and windows via the native window 'storage' event listener within 5 milliseconds.
- Elapsed Time Tracking:
  The 1-second interval recalculates Math.floor((now - new Date(order.createdAt).getTime()) / 1000) continuously.


8. EXPECTED RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Zero paper slips lost, dropped, or soaked with cooking oil.
- Complete kitchen queue visibility: Kitchen supervisors know exactly how many tickets are waiting.
- Overdue ticket alerts: Tickets waiting over 10 minutes turn bright red, prompting immediate expediting.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Strict Stall Isolation: Fast Food orders go ONLY to the Fast Food KDS; Beverage orders go ONLY to the Beverage KDS.
- Revert Capability: If a cook accidentally taps "Mark as Ready" too early, clicking the button again allows reverting the ticket back to Preparing.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can we run POS and KDS on two separate screens?
A1: Yes! Simply open the POS on your front counter device and open the KDS on your kitchen display device. State synchronizes automatically.

Q2: What happens if the kitchen display disconnects from Wi-Fi?
A2: Because FoodDesk uses local-first browser storage on the counter network, existing orders remain visible on screen without crashing.

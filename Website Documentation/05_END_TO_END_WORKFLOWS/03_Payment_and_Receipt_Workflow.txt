1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Payment and Receipt Workflow governs the critical financial transaction and physical proof-of-purchase phase of the FoodDesk counter operation.

Cashiers in food stalls handle rapid transactions involving physical currency notes, coins, and mobile UPI payments. Cashiers must calculate change accurately during high-pressure rushes, verify digital transfers before handing over food, update the order's payment status, and issue a compliant physical receipt.

This workflow provides a unified, fail-safe tender management system:
- Cash Payment Processing: Integrated tendered amount input with real-time change calculation.
- Dynamic UPI QR Code Generation: Generates scannable Bharat QR / UPI codes encoded with the exact bill total and the stall's verified UPI VPA.
- Transaction ID Recording: Option to log bank UTR reference numbers for UPI auditing.
- Compliant 58mm & 80mm Digital Thermal Receipts: Authentic thermal printer layout with tax invoice headers, order numbers, pickup tokens, line items, and browser-native printing.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- From POS Success Modal: Click "Collect Payment Now" immediately after placing an order.
- From Order History Hub: Click the green "Pay" button on any unpaid order row in /orders.
- Direct Receipt Access: Click the printer icon on any paid order row in /orders.


3. SCREEN LAYOUT & MODALS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This workflow operates across two specialized modal dialogues:

1. POS Payment Modal (POSPaymentModal.jsx):
   - Header: Order Number, Stall Name, Grand Total Amount in large bold font.
   - Tender Selector: Segmented tabs for "Cash" vs. "UPI / Online QR".
   - Cash Mode: Amount Tendered input, Quick denomination buttons (+₹100, +₹200, +₹500), and Change Due display.
   - UPI Mode: Dynamic QR code preview, stall UPI VPA address, Payee name, and Transaction Reference ID input.
   - Footer: "Confirm Payment" button.

2. Digital Thermal Receipt Modal (DigitalThermalReceiptModal.jsx):
   - Authentic receipt preview box formatted to match 58mm/80mm thermal paper rolls.
   - Action Buttons: "Print Receipt" (triggers browser print) and "Start Next Order".


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. CASH CALCULATOR ELEMENTS:
- Grand Total Display: The exact amount due in rupees (e.g., "Amount Due: ₹320.00").
- Cash Tendered Input: <input type="number"> with Rupee symbol prefix.
- Quick Denomination Buttons: Clickable pill buttons to quickly add exact note values (+₹100, +₹200, +₹500, Exact Amount).
- Change Due Box:
  - If Tendered >= Due: Emerald green box showing bold amount (e.g., "Change to Return: ₹180.00").
  - If Tendered < Due: Amber box showing remaining balance (e.g., "Remaining: ₹20.00").
- "Confirm Cash Payment" Button: Disabled until Tendered >= Due.

B. UPI QR CODE ELEMENTS:
- Dynamic QR Canvas: High-resolution QR code encoding the standard Indian UPI intent string:
  upi://pay?pa=[StallUPI]&pn=[PayeeName]&am=[GrandTotal]&cu=INR&tn=[OrderNumber]
- UPI ID Callout: Monospace text showing the stall's handle (e.g., "fastfood@upi").
- Payee Name: Registered account name (e.g., "Fast Food Enterprises").
- Bank Reference ID Input (Optional): <input type="text"> for recording the 12-digit UPI UTR number.
- "Verify & Confirm UPI Payment" Button: Records payment as successful.

C. THERMAL RECEIPT ELEMENTS:
- Receipt Header: Center-aligned Stall Name, Address, Contact Number, and "TAX INVOICE / CASH MEMO".
- Prominent Token Box: Extra-large Pickup Token (e.g., "#102") centered at the top.
- Order Details: Order Number (ORD-YYYYMMDD-XXXX), Date, Time, Cashier Name.
- Itemized Table: Description, Qty, Rate, Total.
- Financial Summary: Subtotal, Discount Applied, Net Grand Total, Payment Mode (CASH or UPI), Tendered & Change.
- Footer Message: "Thank you for dining with us! Track your order live at: [URL]".
- Print Button: Full-width button with Printer icon.


5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Select Payment Tender: Toggle between Cash and UPI.
2. Enter Cash Amount: Type currency tendered or use quick denomination buttons.
3. Verify Cash Change: Read the exact change to return to the customer.
4. Scan UPI QR Code: Present the dynamic QR code for the customer to scan with PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or BHIM.
5. Record Bank Reference: Enter the 12-digit UTR number for digital audit reconciliation.
6. Print Thermal Receipt: Click "Print Receipt" to output to an Epson, TVS, or thermal POS printer.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SCENARIO A: CASH TRANSACTION WITH CHANGE CALCULATION
1. An order of ₹320.00 is placed. In the Success Modal, click "Collect Payment Now".
2. Confirm that the "Cash" tab is selected.
3. The customer hands the cashier a ₹500 note.
4. Cashier clicks the "+₹500" quick button (or types "500").
5. The calculator instantly highlights in green: "Change to Return: ₹180.00".
6. Cashier hands ₹180 in cash to the customer.
7. Cashier clicks "Confirm Cash Payment".
8. Payment is recorded. The Thermal Receipt modal opens automatically!

SCENARIO B: DYNAMIC UPI QR PAYMENT
1. An order of ₹240.00 is placed. Click "Collect Payment Now".
2. Click the "UPI / Online QR" tab.
3. A crisp, dynamic QR code generates on screen encoding exact ₹240.00 for fastfood@upi.
4. The customer opens Google Pay (or PhonePe) and scans the screen.
5. The customer's phone immediately shows: "Pay ₹240 to Fast Food Enterprises (Order ORD-20261116-0002)".
6. Customer authorizes the payment via UPI PIN. Customer shows the successful green checkmark on their phone.
7. Cashier types the last 4 digits of the UTR into the reference box (optional).
8. Cashier clicks "Verify & Confirm UPI Payment".
9. Order is marked as "PAID" via UPI. Thermal receipt opens for printing!


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Payment Ledger Creation (stallDataService.recordPayment):
  1. Generates unique payment record: { id, stallId, orderId, amount, method, status: 'successful', timestamp, referenceId }.
  2. Updates order.paymentStatus = 'paid'.
  3. Updates order.paymentMethod = 'cash' or 'upi'.
  4. Dispatches custom event: window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('fooddesk:payment-completed', { detail: payment })).
  5. Dispatches window 'storage' event for multi-screen sync.
- Financial Realization:
  The order is now recognized as realized revenue in Financial Analytics. Gross sales, net revenue, and cash/UPI tender distributions update immediately.
- Thermal Receipt Generator (stallDataService.getReceiptData):
  Compiles frozen snapshots of items, discounts, token, and payments into a printable receipt data structure.

10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Insufficient Tender Prevention:
  In Cash mode, the "Confirm Cash Payment" button remains disabled if the entered amount is less than the Grand Total.
- Non-Destructive Payment History:
  If an order is later refunded, the original payment record remains in the ledger alongside a corresponding negative refund entry.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can I reprint a receipt if the customer asks for one later?
A1: Yes! Open the Order Management Hub (/orders), find the order, and click the printer icon. You can reprint receipts at any time.

Q2: Does the thermal receipt print properly on 58mm narrow paper rolls?
A2: Yes! The receipt CSS includes dedicated print stylesheets that automatically constrain content to 48mm-58mm roll widths with clean text wrapping.

1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Menu and Product Workflow governs the complete operational lifecycle of food and beverage products within FoodDesk.

A successful food stall must continuously adapt its menu: introducing seasonal specials, adjusting prices in response to ingredient cost inflation, analyzing gross profit margins, immediately toggling items as "Sold Out" when prep ingredients run low, and discontinuing unpopular dishes without destroying historical sales ledgers.

This workflow coordinates product lifecycle operations:
1. Product Creation & Pricing Economics: Defining selling prices, cost baselines (COGS), and evaluating live unit margins.
2. Category Organization: Structuring catalog sections for intuitive POS and customer browsing.
3. Rapid Stockout Toggling: 1-click availability control during peak service rushes.
4. Product Details Editing: Updating recipes, descriptions, or prices.
5. Non-Destructive Soft Archival: Removing obsolete items from the active POS catalog while 100% preserving historical order records.


2. HOW TO ACCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Workstation URL: Menu Management at http://localhost:5173/menu
- Sidebar Navigation: Click "Menu Management" (UtensilsCrossed icon).


3. SCREEN LAYOUT & STAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This workflow progresses through four administrative touchpoints:
- Stage 1: Active Menu Catalog Table / Grid (/menu).
- Stage 2: Category Management Modal.
- Stage 3: Menu Item Creation & Editing Modal (with live margin calculator).
- Stage 4: Archive Confirmation Modal.


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key operational elements involved in this workflow:
- Live Gross Margin Calculator: Displays unit profit in rupees (+₹XX.00) and margin percentage ((profit/price)*100) dynamically as prices are typed.
- 1-Click Availability Toggle: Green "In Stock" vs. Yellow "Sold Out" button.
- Dietary Selector: Pure Vegetarian (Green) vs. Non-Vegetarian (Red) toggle.
- Category Selector: Dropdown to pick existing categories or create new ones.
- Soft Archive Action: Preserves past order references while hiding from POS.


5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Create new food item with price, cost, and dietary tags.
2. Review live unit profit margin before publishing.
3. Organize items into categories (Burgers, Rolls, Drinks, etc.).
4. Instantly mark an item as "Sold Out" when kitchen runs out.
5. Restore a sold-out item to "In Stock" when prepped.
6. Edit item price or description.
7. Soft archive a discontinued dish.
8. Restore an archived dish for a new season.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: CREATING A NEW DISH WITH MARGIN ANALYSIS
1. In Menu Management (/menu), click "Add New Item".
2. Enter Name: "Paneer Tikka Roll".
3. Category: Select "Rolls".
4. Dietary: Select "Veg".
5. Enter Selling Price: "150".
6. Enter Cost Price (COGS): "60".
7. Live Calculator immediately displays: "Gross Profit: +₹90.00 | Margin: 60%".
8. Owner confirms this meets the target 55%+ food margin.
9. Enter Description: "Char-grilled paneer wrapped in flaky paratha".
10. Click "Create Menu Item".
11. The dish is immediately live on POS and public customer menus!

PHASE 2: RUSH-HOUR STOCKOUT MANAGEMENT
12. At 8:00 PM, the kitchen announces: "We have run out of paneer for tonight!".
13. Cashier opens /menu.
14. Cashier locates "Paneer Tikka Roll" and clicks the green "In Stock" button.
15. Button immediately turns yellow reading "Sold Out".
16. RESULT: At the POS workstation, the item is dimmed and blocked from carts. On the customer's phone menu, it displays a yellow "Sold Out" overlay. Zero customer frustration!

PHASE 3: NEXT MORNING RESTOCK
17. The next morning, a fresh paneer delivery arrives.
18. Staff opens /menu and clicks the yellow "Sold Out" button on "Paneer Tikka Roll".
19. It turns green ("In Stock"). Instantly orderable again!

PHASE 4: DISCONTINUING AN ITEM SAFELY
20. At the end of the year, a slow-selling dish is discontinued.
21. On that dish's card, click the Archive icon.
22. Modal confirms: "Archiving removes this item from the active POS menu. All past orders and historical receipts will remain 100% intact."
23. Click "Archive Item".
24. Item is archived safely. Historical P&L reports and receipts never break!


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-Time Event Dispatch:
  Toggling availability or adding an item dispatches:
  window.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('fooddesk:menu_updated')) and 'storage'.
- Cross-Screen Reactivity:
  POS terminals and customer phones update their catalog displays in under 5 milliseconds without page reloads.
- Immutable Historical Integrity:
  Archiving or editing an item modifies ONLY future catalog queries. Historical orders in storage maintain their frozen snapshot sellingPrice and costPrice.


8. EXPECTED RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Instant stockout enforcement across counter and customer screens.
- Healthy menu economics: Owners visualize profit margins before setting prices.
- Safe menu iteration: Zero risk of orphan records or broken historical receipts.


9. RELATED MODULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Module 4: Counter POS (displays active items and respects sold-out flags).
- Module 11: Financial Analytics (uses item costPrice snapshot for COGS).
- Module 13: Public Customer Digital Menu (displays active catalog to patrons).


10. RESTRICTIONS AND IMPORTANT NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hard Deletion Prohibited: Items cannot be hard-deleted because past orders reference their ID. Soft archiving is strictly enforced.
- Cross-Stall Isolation: Fast Food menu edits cannot affect Beverages or Desserts.


11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: If I increase the price of an item from ₹120 to ₹140, will past sales change?
A1: No! Past orders preserve the frozen ₹120 price they were purchased at. Only orders placed after the price change will use ₹140.

Q2: Can I restore an archived item if I decide to bring it back next month?
A2: Yes! Open the "Archived" tab in Menu Management, find the item, and click "Restore Item". It will return to the active POS catalog immediately.

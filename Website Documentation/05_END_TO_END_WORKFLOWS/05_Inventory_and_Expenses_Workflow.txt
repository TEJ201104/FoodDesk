1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Inventory and Expenses Workflow governs back-of-house cost control, raw material stock tracking, and operating expenditure accounting in FoodDesk.

In the informal food stall industry, untracked cash outlays for groceries and unrecorded ingredient spoilage represent the two biggest sources of hidden business failure. If a stall owner buys ₹5,000 of vegetables in cash from the wholesale mandi and later throws out ₹1,000 of spoiled paneer without logging either event, their financial reports become completely fictional.

This workflow provides an integrated back-of-house loop:
1. Stock Intakes: Receiving raw ingredients and recording initial or restock quantities.
2. Threshold Monitoring: Receiving visual warnings when ingredients hit reorder levels.
3. Wastage Auditing: Logging spoiled, expired, or burned food with formal reason codes.
4. Expenditure Recording: Logging cash and digital outlays for materials, utilities, and labor.
5. P&L Reconciliation: Automatically feeding expense deductions into net profit reports.

- Expense Console: http://localhost:5173/expenses (Sidebar: "Expenses").


3. SCREEN LAYOUT & STAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This workflow coordinates across two dedicated modules:
- Module 9: Inventory Management Hub (/inventory) with Active Ingredients, Wastage Log, and Movement History tabs.
- Module 10: Expense Management Hub (/expenses) with 8 cost categories, breakdown charts, and audit histories.


4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key operational elements involved in this workflow:
- Low-Stock Alert Banner: Amber callout alerting staff when ingredients fall below minThreshold.
- Stock Adjustment Modal: Modal to execute Add Stock (+) or Reduce Stock (-) with reason codes.
- Wastage Incident Logger: Captures spoiled quantities, reason category, and incident notes.
- Operating Expense Modal: Captures Date, Category, Rupee Amount, Description, and Recorder.
- Versioned Audit Trails: Immutable records of every revision or deletion.


5. AVAILABLE ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Add new ingredient to tracking registry.
2. Execute manual stock adjustment (restock or count reconciliation).
3. Record ingredient wastage (spoilage, expiry, prep loss).
4. Review complete chronological stock movement audit log.
5. Record cash or digital operating expense.
6. Categorize expenses across 8 standardized classifications.
7. Inspect versioned expense audit history.
8. Soft delete duplicate or erroneous expense records with mandatory reason.


6. STEP-BY-STEP USAGE GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1: RECEIVING INGREDIENTS & LOGGING PURCHASE EXPENSE
1. Wholesale delivery arrives with 15 kg of Paneer and 10 kg of Butter. Total bill: ₹6,500 cash.
2. STEP A — LOG INGREDIENT RESTOCK:
   - Open /inventory.
   - On "Paneer" row, click "Adjust". Select "Add Stock (+)", enter "15", reason "Wholesale Delivery". Confirm.
   - On "Butter" row, click "Adjust". Select "Add Stock (+)", enter "10", reason "Wholesale Delivery". Confirm.
   - Both stock levels update immediately.
3. STEP B — LOG CASH EXPENSE:
   - Open /expenses.
   - Click "Record Expense".
   - Category: Select "Raw Materials".
   - Amount: Enter "6500".
   - Description: "Wholesale Dairy Delivery: 15kg Paneer + 10kg Butter (Bill #D-102)".
   - Click "Save Expense Record".
   - Net profit for the month adjusts immediately to account for this ₹6,500 cost.

PHASE 2: DETECTING LOW STOCK & RESTOCKING
4. Later in the week, staff opens /inventory.
5. The amber Low-Stock Alert Banner is displayed: "Cooking Oil is below minimum threshold (2 litres remaining; min threshold: 5 litres)".
6. Staff orders a 15-litre tin of cooking oil, preventing a disastrous counter stockout during Friday dinner rush.

PHASE 3: RECORDING UNPREVENTABLE WASTAGE
7. During food prep, a cook accidentally burns a batch of 2 kg of onions.
8. In /inventory, click "Record Wastage".
9. Select "Onions", Quantity: "2", Reason: "Prep Loss / Burned", Notes: "Burned during high-heat saute prep".
10. Confirm. Onion stock reduces by 2 kg, and a permanent entry is added to the Wastage Log.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Prototype Inventory Rule:
  In accordance with PRD Section 9, POS sales do not automatically decrement raw ingredients. Inventory tracking operates via manual periodic stock takes, stock adjustments, and wastage logs.
- Dual-Entry Ledger Impact:
  Logging an expense directly increases Total Operating Expenses and reduces Net Profit in Financial Analytics.
  Logging wastage increments the cumulative wastage counter and documents operational shrinkage.


8. EXPECTED RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Zero surprise stockouts: Minimum thresholds trigger high-visibility warnings.
- Clean audit trails: Every stock adjustment and expense revision is permanently tracked with timestamps and user names.

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Why should I log both a Stock Adjustment and an Expense?
A1: Stock Adjustment records the physical weight of ingredients in the kitchen (e.g., +15 kg Paneer). The Expense records the financial cash outflow (e.g., -₹6,500 Cash). Both are essential for complete operational and accounting control.

Q2: Can I edit an expense if I typed the wrong amount?
A2: Yes! Click the Edit (Pencil) icon on the expense row, correct the amount, and save. FoodDesk automatically creates a revision entry in the audit trail.

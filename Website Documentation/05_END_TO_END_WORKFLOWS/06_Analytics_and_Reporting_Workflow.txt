1. FEATURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Analytics and Reporting Workflow governs executive financial decision-making, commercial performance auditing, and tax reporting in FoodDesk.

At the end of a business day, week, month, or fiscal quarter, stall owners and financial controllers must answer essential business questions:
- What was our true net profit after deducting all raw material costs and daily stall expenses?
- How much revenue came in through cash versus dynamic UPI QR transfers?
- Which 5 menu items drove our highest profit margins, and which items are lagging?
- How do we export clean financial spreadsheets for our accountant or tax consultant?

This workflow coordinates the complete analytical review cycle:
1. Date Range Boundary Selection: Isolating reporting periods in Asia/Kolkata timezone.
2. Core P&L Audit: Reviewing Gross Sales, Net Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit, Expenses, and True Net Profit.
3. Tender Verification: Inspecting the Cash vs. UPI distribution pie chart.
4. Menu Contribution Analysis: Evaluating best-selling vs. least-selling dishes.
5. Multi-Format Output: Exporting structured CSV files and printing formatted A4 executive reports.

4. DETAILED ELEMENT-BY-ELEMENT EXPLANATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key operational elements involved in this workflow:
- Date Range Presets: Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, This Year, Custom Range.
- Authoritative P&L Formula Engine:
  - Gross Sales = Sum(Item Selling Price * Qty) for paid orders.
  - Net Revenue = Gross Sales - Discounts - Refunds.
  - COGS = Sum(Frozen Item Cost Price * Qty) for paid orders.
  - Gross Profit = Net Revenue - COGS.
  - Total Expenses = Sum(Operating Expenses in period).
  - True Net Profit = Gross Profit - Total Expenses.
- Payment Distribution Pie Chart: Shows percentage and rupee shares for Cash, Dynamic UPI, Static QR, Other.
- Top 5 Best-Sellers Bar Chart: Ranks dishes by volume and revenue contribution.
- CSV Exporter: Generates FoodDesk_[StallName]_[Period]_Report.csv.
- Printable Report Modal: Standard A4 report layout with browser print optimization.

PHASE 1: DEFINING THE REPORTING PERIOD

3. The toolbar label confirms: "01 Nov 2026 to 16 Nov 2026".

PHASE 2: AUDITING THE PROFIT & LOSS STATEMENT
4. Review Row 1 of the KPI Grid:
   - Gross Sales: ₹42,500.00 (Total face value of orders placed).
   - Net Revenue: ₹40,100.00 (After ₹1,800 discounts and ₹600 refunds).
   - COGS: ₹18,200.00 (Direct raw food cost based on frozen snapshots).
5. Review Row 2 of the KPI Grid:
   - Gross Profit: ₹21,900.00 (Net Revenue - COGS). Gross Margin: 55%.
   - Operating Expenses: ₹12,400.00 (Gas, rent, electricity, salaries, packaging).
   - True Net Profit: ₹9,500.00 (Gross Profit - Expenses). Net Margin: 24%.

PHASE 3: AUDITING TENDER & PAYMENT MODES
6. Scroll to the "Payment Methods Breakdown" chart.
7. Observe that Dynamic UPI accounted for 65% of collections (₹26,065.00) and Cash accounted for 35% (₹14,035.00).
8. Verify that the physical cash in the till matches ₹14,035.00 minus any cash expenses logged today.

PHASE 4: MENU OPTIMIZATION REVIEW
9. Check the "Top 5 Best-Selling Items" chart:
   - "Veg Burger" is #1: 120 units sold, contributing ₹14,400 revenue with a 54% margin.

PHASE 5: REPORT GENERATION & SHARING
11. Click "Export CSV".
    - The browser downloads the complete report spreadsheet. Email it to your accountant.
12. Click "Print Report".
    - The A4 report preview appears with formal corporate headers and authorized signature lines.
    - Click "Print Report" to output to paper or save as a PDF document.


7. SYSTEM BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Integer Paise Calculation Engine:
  All monetary additions, subtractions, discounts, and percentage allocations execute using integer paise math (1 INR = 100 paise) via analyticsService, eliminating IEEE-754 floating-point rounding errors.
- Unpaid & Failed Order Exclusion:
  Orders with paymentStatus: 'pending' or 'failed' are strictly excluded from Gross Sales and Net Revenue.
- Frozen Snapshot Costing:
  COGS calculations use the immutable costPrice snapshot recorded in each order line-item at checkout time, ensuring future menu price updates never corrupt past accounting records.

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: Can I export analytics for a single day?
A1: Yes! Click "Today" (or "Yesterday", or pick a single date under "Custom Range") and click "Export CSV".

Q2: Why does Net Revenue differ from Gross Sales?
A2: Gross Sales is the original face-value total of all food ordered. Net Revenue is the actual cash collected after deducting discounts given to customers and refunds returned to customers.

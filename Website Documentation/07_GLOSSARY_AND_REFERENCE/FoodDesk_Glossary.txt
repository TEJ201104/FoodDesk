4. DETAILED ALPHABETICAL GLOSSARY (A TO Z)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ A ]
- AuthGuard:
  A client-side React Router route interceptor (located in frontend/src/routes/ProtectedRoute.jsx) that blocks unauthenticated visitors from accessing internal stall or administrator URLs, redirecting them safely to /login.
- Atomic Order Creation:
  A database execution principle ensuring that an entire order transaction (order number allocation, token generation, line-item snapshotting, financial computation, and storage write) succeeds completely or fails completely, preventing orphan or corrupted records.

[ B ]
- Back-of-House (BOH):
  The commercial kitchen and storage operations area of a food stall (including line cooks, prep tables, refrigerators, fryers, and dishwashing) hidden from customer view. In FoodDesk, BOH operations are managed via the Kitchen Display System (KDS) and Inventory pages.
- Bharat QR / UPI QR:
  Standardized Indian digital payment QR specifications enabling customers to scan with mobile apps (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) to transfer money directly into the merchant's bank account.

[ C ]
- COGS (Cost of Goods Sold):
  The direct material cost of raw ingredients required to produce a dish. In FoodDesk, COGS is recorded as costPrice in Menu Management and frozen as an immutable snapshot inside each order ticket at checkout time.
  Formula: Total COGS = Sum(item.costPrice * item.quantity).
- Cash Drawer / Till:
  The physical cash box at the counter where cash notes and coins are stored. FoodDesk provides an automated Cash Calculator showing exact change to return to the customer.

[ D ]
- Digital Thermal Receipt:
  A formatted, electronic proof-of-purchase slip formatted specifically to match 58mm or 80mm roll widths on commercial POS thermal receipt printers.
- Double-Key Isolation:
  A security architecture used on public tracking pages requiring both a valid stall slug AND an authorized order number (e.g., /track/:stallSlug/:orderNumber) to prevent customers from guessing other stalls' order records.

[ E ]
- Elapsed Wait Timer:
  A real-time 1-second dynamic counter displayed on each KDS card showing the exact minutes and seconds that have elapsed since the customer placed the order at the counter.
- Expediter (Expo):
  The kitchen or counter staff member responsible for inspecting plated food trays, matching them to pickup tokens, and handing orders over to waiting customers.

[ F ]
- FIFO (First In, First Out):
  An operational kitchen queuing principle where orders that arrive first are cooked and served first. FoodDesk arranges KDS columns strictly in chronological FIFO sequence.
- Frozen Snapshot:
  An architectural mechanism where the selling price and cost price of an item are deeply copied into an order record at checkout. Even if the owner later edits menu prices in the catalog, historical sales records remain 100% frozen.

[ G ]
- GMV (Gross Merchandising Volume):
  The total cumulative value of all food and beverage sales completed across every stall operating on the platform. Monitored globally by the Super Administrator on the Platform Dashboard.
- Gross Profit:
  The money remaining from food sales after subtracting direct ingredient costs (COGS), before deducting general operating expenses.
  Formula: Gross Profit = Net Revenue - COGS.

[ I ]
- IST (Indian Standard Time / Asia/Kolkata):
  The canonical timezone anchor (UTC+05:30) used by FoodDesk for all date boundaries, sales reporting filters, clock displays, and audit logs.
- Integer Paise Math:
  A monetary calculation architecture where currency amounts are multiplied by 100 and stored as whole integers (paise) to eliminate floating-point rounding errors.

[ K ]
- KDS (Kitchen Display System):
  A digital Kanban screen (Module 6) mounted in commercial kitchens that organizes incoming counter tickets across 4 visual stages: Received, Preparing, Ready for Pickup, and Completed.

[ L ]
- Local-First Architecture:
  A software design philosophy where an application stores and queries its database directly on the local device (in this prototype, browser localStorage) to guarantee zero latency and offline resilience.

[ M ]
- Minimum Reorder Threshold (minThreshold):
  The safe minimum quantity of a raw material ingredient (e.g., 5 kg). When stock drops to or below this level, FoodDesk triggers automated low-stock warnings.
- Multi-Stall Tenancy:
  The capability of a single business owner account (like Owner 1) to own and operate several food stalls independently while maintaining complete data separation between them.

[ N ]
- Net Revenue:
  The actual, realized money collected from food sales after subtracting discounts and refunds.
  Formula: Net Revenue = Gross Sales - Total Discounts - Total Refunds.
- Net Profit:
  The true bottom-line profit earned by the stall after subtracting food ingredient costs (COGS) AND operating expenses (gas, electricity, rent, labor, packaging).
  Formula: Net Profit = Gross Profit - Total Operating Expenses.

[ P ]
- P&L (Profit and Loss Statement):
  The standard financial accounting report detailing revenue, cost of goods sold, operating expenses, and net profit over a specific time period.
- Pickup Token:
  A prominent 3-digit sequential number (#101 to #999) assigned to every counter order, printed on the receipt, displayed on the KDS, and used by staff to call out customer orders.
- POS (Point of Sale):
  The front-of-house counter cash register terminal (Module 4) used by cashiers to take walk-in orders, apply discounts, and collect payment.

[ Q ]
- QSR (Quick-Service Restaurant):
  Commercial food establishments characterized by walk-in counter ordering, rapid food prep, and lack of table service. The primary target market for FoodDesk.

[ R ]
- RoleGuard:
  A client-side security router (in ProtectedRoute.jsx) enforcing strict separation between Stall Owners and Super Admins, preventing unauthorized role crossover.

[ S ]
- Soft Archiving:
  A data preservation technique where an item is marked isArchived = true instead of being deleted, hiding it from active POS menus while keeping historical receipts and P&L reports 100% intact.
- StallGuard:
  A client-side runtime route interceptor (in StallGuard.jsx) that continuously audits active stall ownership and self-heals client storage if manual DevTools tampering is detected.

[ T ]
- Tender Method:
  The payment mode used by a customer to settle their bill (Cash, Dynamic UPI, Static QR, Split Tender).

[ U ]
- UTR (Unique Transaction Reference):
  A 12-digit bank reference number generated upon successful completion of an Indian UPI digital payment. Recorded optionally in FoodDesk for digital audit trails.

[ W ]
- Web Locks API:
  A W3C browser concurrency standard (navigator.locks) used by FoodDesk to coordinate atomic writes across multiple open browser tabs, preventing race conditions.

11. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: What is the difference between an Order Number and a Pickup Token?
A1: An Order Number (e.g., ORD-20261116-0001) is a long, unique, legal tax invoice identifier used for bookkeeping and tracking. A Pickup Token (e.g., #101) is a short, memorable 3-digit number used by staff to shout out orders at a noisy counter.

Q2: What is the difference between Gross Sales and Net Revenue?
A2: Gross Sales is the original face value of all food items ordered. Net Revenue is what you actually collected after deducting discounts given to customers and refunds returned to customers.
